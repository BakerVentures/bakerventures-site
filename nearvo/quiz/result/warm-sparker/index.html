A connection style

The Warm Sparker

You feel closest through warmth, play and touch.

Affection is your language: hugs, hand-holding, laughing at something silly, a playful nudge in the kitchen. Lightness and physical closeness help you feel connected and help you reset after a rough patch.

Worth knowing: not everyone feels safe to be playful when they are stressed. Match your partner’s mood, then lift it gently.

Three things to try this week, together

  1. Start or end each day this week with a proper ten-second hug.
  2. Invent one tiny silly ritual together, like a handshake, a nickname or a kitchen dance.
  3. Hold hands on a walk, and swap who sets the pace.

A question to ask your partner tonight

What kind of touch or playfulness feels best to you when we are both relaxed?
