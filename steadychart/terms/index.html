What Steadychart is — and the line it does not cross

General wellness only: no diagnosis, no flare prediction, no medication or dosing advice.

Steadychart is a daily symptom log. You pick one overall rating for the day, tap a severity for each symptom you track, and optionally add a note. It shows that back to you as history, a 14-day chart, and plain comparisons, and it can export what you logged. That is the entire product.

This is the same thing the app tells you in-product, and these terms repeat it rather than restate it differently, so the two can never drift apart:

  • On the Insights tab: "Not medical advice — just a plain comparison of what you've logged. This never diagnoses, predicts, or tells you what to do about a symptom."
  • On every insight the app produces: "This isn't a diagnosis or a prediction, and a difference here doesn't mean one thing led to the other."
  • In the footer of every clinician PDF you export: "This is a self-reported log, not a diagnosis. It is not validated or clinically proven and makes no medical claims."
  • On first launch: "There is nothing here that predicts, diagnoses, or advises on treatment — just what you choose to log."

Said plainly, and as a term of this agreement:

  • It does not diagnose anything, does not predict flares or any other future event, and does not give dosing or medication advice of any kind.
  • It is not a substitute for a clinician. Nothing in the app is a professional opinion, and using it creates no doctor-patient or other professional relationship.
  • The comparisons in Insights are arithmetic over what you typed. They are our own summary of your own records, not a clinical instrument, and a difference between two numbers is not evidence that one thing caused the other.
