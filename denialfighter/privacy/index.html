Privacy Policy for Denial Fighter

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Denial Fighter (a working name; the final app name is not yet chosen) is an on-device health-insurance and medical-bill appeal assistant built by Baker Ventures LLC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. The short version

Denial Fighter stores everything you enter on your own device. We do not operate a server that receives your claims, letters, or personal information, and we do not sell or share your data, because we never have a copy of it to begin with.

2. What we collect, how, and what it is used for

  • Claims you enter (plan name, member name, claim number, denial date, provider, service description, amount billed, notes) are saved in a local database on your device, not on a server we operate or can access. They are used to track your appeal and to fill in the draft letters you generate.
  • Local reminders are scheduled entirely on your device, to alert you before an appeal deadline. Reminder text never includes your claim details — see Section 3.

That is the complete list. In particular, the app has nothing to sell: it is free, contains no in-app purchase, and so never reads a purchase record, a receipt, or a store account from Apple, Google, or any purchase service.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, health information, financial information, or claim details on any server. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across apps or websites. If that changes in a future version, this policy — and the app’s Privacy Nutrition Label and Data safety section — will be updated to say exactly what changed and why.

3. Notifications

Deadline reminders are generic by design (“N days left on an appeal deadline — open the app for details”) and never include your claim label, denial type, provider name, or any other claim-specific text, even though the app has that information locally. This is deliberate: a locked-screen notification is not a private place to show health or billing details.

4. Who we share your data with

Your claim data is shared with no one — it never leaves your device, so there is no recipient at all. The app is free with nothing to unlock, so it has no reason to talk to Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or any other purchase or analytics service, and it doesn’t.

5. Data retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

  • Retention. Claims stay on your device until you delete them. Baker Ventures LLC holds no copy and has no retention period of its own.
  • Deletion. Deleting a claim in the app removes it, and its reminders, from your device immediately. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored, including its local database. There is no account to close and no company-held record to request the deletion of.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can turn off notification permission at any time in your device Settings, which stops deadline reminders. Because no claim data is transmitted, there is no other processing by us to withdraw consent for.

6. Not legal, medical, or insurance advice

Using this app does not create any professional relationship. See the in-app disclaimer, shown on every screen with denial-type guidance or a generated letter.

7. Changes to this policy

We’ll update the date above and describe what changed whenever this policy changes.
