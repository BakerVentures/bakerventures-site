How to text a girl

Texting cannot make someone like you. It can keep existing interest alive and make meeting sound appealing.

Last updated 2026-08-20

Texting cannot make someone like you. It can fail to kill interest that already exists, and it can make meeting up sound appealing. That means: match her pace, give her something to react to rather than a question to answer, keep messages roughly her length, and move toward meeting sooner than feels comfortable. Most texting advice fails because it aims at the wrong target.

What texting is actually for

It is a bridge, not a destination. The purpose is to get from matched to meeting with the interest intact, and almost every common mistake comes from treating text as the place where attraction gets built.

That reframe fixes a lot on its own. You stop trying to be impressive, stop drafting messages for ten minutes, and stop needing every exchange to go well. You just need it to not go badly and to end up somewhere.

The rules that carry most of it

  • Match her pace and length. Twice her word count reads as more invested than she is. A third reads as disinterested.
  • Give her something to react to. A statement with a hook beats a question. Questions make her do the work.
  • Be specific. Anything you could have sent to anyone tells her she is talking to nobody in particular.
  • Do not deliberately delay. People notice, and what they notice is the tactic.
  • Move to meeting. Text has a shelf life. Conversations that live in the app too long tend to die there.

Texting a girl you just met

Say where you met inside the first message. Making her reconstruct who you are is an unnecessary hurdle at the exact moment she has the least invested.

Then give her one specific thing from the interaction to pick up. A callback to a joke, a thing she mentioned, a mild continuation of whatever you were talking about. That is the whole message — short, identified, and easy to answer.

Timing: within a day or two. The three-day rule is from a different era of dating and now mostly signals that you are not that interested.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you text a girl you just met?

Reference where you met within the first message so she knows who you are without having to work it out. Keep it short, give her something specific to react to, and text within a day or two — waiting three days is advice from a different era and mostly reads as disinterest now.

How often should you text her?

Roughly match her pace. If she replies within an hour, replying within an hour is normal. If she takes a day, taking a day is normal. Deliberately delaying replies to seem busy is a strategy people notice, and what they notice is that you are running a strategy.

What do you text a girl to keep her interested?

Things she can react to rather than things she has to answer. Opinions, callbacks to earlier messages, light teasing, and specifics beat questions about her weekend. Interest is sustained by the conversation being enjoyable, not by any individual message being clever.

How do you text a girl to make her like you?

You mostly cannot, and chasing that framing is why a lot of texting goes badly. What text can do is fail to kill existing interest, and give enough of a sense of you that meeting sounds appealing. Aim for that and move to meeting sooner rather than later.

Is it bad to double text?

No. One follow-up is normal and often works. The problem is the third message, and any message that comments on being ignored rather than giving her something new to reply to.
