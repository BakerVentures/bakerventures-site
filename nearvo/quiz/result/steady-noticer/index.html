A connection style

The Steady Noticer

You feel closest when someone has paid attention.

You tend to feel loved through the small, specific things: a remembered detail, a text that shows they were thinking of you, an unspoken “I can tell you’re tired.” You probably notice a lot about your partner too, often more than you say out loud.

Worth knowing: if you wait to be noticed, your partner may not realize what you are hoping for. Saying it once, kindly, can help.

Three things to try this week, together

  1. Each day this week, tell your partner one small thing you noticed about them. Keep it specific.
  2. Pick one detail from a past conversation and bring it back with something that nods to it.
  3. Take turns finishing the sentence “A small thing you do that I love is…”.

A question to ask your partner tonight

What is something small I did recently that you noticed and liked?
