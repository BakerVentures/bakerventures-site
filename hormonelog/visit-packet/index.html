How to Prepare a Symptom Log for a Clinician Visit

A short visit packet helps you bring your own observations and questions to an appointment. You can prepare one with paper or any notes app. It is a conversation aid for your clinician to interpret.

Not medical advice. This page does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment or give dosing guidance. Discuss health questions and medication changes with a qualified clinician. A log cannot determine the cause of a symptom.

What to include

  1. The period covered. Put the start and end dates at the top, along with what you want to discuss.
  2. A dated symptom timeline. Describe what you noticed, when, and how it affected sleep, work or daily activities. Distinguish observations from guesses about the cause.
  3. A consistent description of impact. Use your own words, such as “woke twice” or “missed an activity.” Note days you did not record rather than treating them as symptom-free.
  4. Your current medication and supplement list. Bring the names and information supplied by your prescriber or labels. Note changes to discuss; this packet should not tell you to change anything.
  5. Your priorities and questions. Choose the few topics you most want the clinician to address.

A one-page outline you can copy

Blank visit-packet outline

Dates covered: __________
Main reason for this visit: __________
Most disruptive observations: __________
Dates / frequency / effect on daily life: __________
Current medication list attached: __________
Recent changes to discuss: __________
Questions I want to ask: __________
Notes and agreed next steps from the visit: __________

Keep the packet readable and private

Lead with a short summary and keep detailed entries behind it. Label any gaps and avoid treating a pattern as proof of a cause. Review the packet before sharing; use the clinic’s preferred secure method and include only the information you intend to share.

Questions to take with you

  • Which observations would be most useful to keep recording?
  • What should I bring to the next appointment?
  • How should I contact the clinic with follow-up questions?
