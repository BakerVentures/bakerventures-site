See every share you'll vest, and when.

rsuvest turns your grant letters into a clear vesting schedule and running value, so you always know what's vested, what's coming, and what it's worth.

rsuvest · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

All your grants, one schedule.Enter each RSU or equity grant and rsuvest builds a single timeline of every vesting date across all of them.
Know what's vested vs. unvested.See at a glance how many shares are already yours and how many are still ahead — no more digging through the equity portal.
Value at today's price.Enter a share price and rsuvest shows the current value of your vested and unvested shares so the schedule means something in dollars.
Upcoming-vest reminders.Get a heads-up before a vesting date so it never sneaks past you — useful for planning around sales windows and taxes.
Plan across offers and refreshes.Track grants from multiple employers or refresh cycles side by side to see your whole equity picture.

How it works

  1. Add a grant — shares, grant date, and vesting schedule (cliff and intervals).
  2. Set a share price to see current value.
  3. Watch it vest — track vested vs. upcoming and get reminded before each date.

Questions

Is rsuvest tax or financial advice?
No. It is a tracking and planning tool and does not provide tax, investment, or financial advice. Talk to a CPA or advisor about decisions and taxes.
Does it show my exact tax withholding?
No. rsuvest tracks shares and value; it does not calculate your specific tax liability, which depends on your income, jurisdiction, and employer withholding.
Does it connect to my brokerage or employer?
No. You enter grant details yourself; the app does not link to brokerages or equity portals.
Does the value update with the live stock price?
You set the share price to value against; see the app for any live-price feature. Private-company shares have no public price, so you enter an estimate.
Is my equity data private?
Your grants are stored for your use and are not sold. See the in-app privacy policy.
Can it handle both cliff and monthly/quarterly vesting?
Yes. You define the cliff and the vesting interval per grant.
Please note. rsuvest is a tracking and planning tool. It does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice. Value and withholding figures are estimates calculated from inputs you enter yourself — including the share price — and any tax figure uses a simplified flat rate, not your real tax situation, so it is a ballpark for planning and not what you will actually owe. Real withholding depends on your employer, your income, and your jurisdiction. Your grant documents and your equity administrator are the authority on your grants; talk to a CPA or tax professional about taxes and to an advisor about decisions.
