About this page. Hatch timing varies by year, elevation and water temperature. Months here are typical ranges from the Hatch Window curated chart, a starting point and not a live report of what is hatching today. Hatch Window is a free fly-fishing companion app from Baker Ventures LLC with a built-in version of this chart, plus streamflow from USGS and weather from Open-Meteo.
