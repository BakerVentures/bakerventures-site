Couples connection style quiz

How do you and your partner each feel closest? Twelve quick questions, five styles, and a few ideas to try together.

For fun and reflection. This is not therapy, counseling or a diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for a professional. It runs in your browser: no account, no email, no cookies, nothing stored or sent.

Twelve quick questions. About two minutes.

This quiz needs JavaScript. Without it, here are the five styles it can show you:

  • The Steady Noticer: closest through attention to small details.
  • The Open Talker: closest through words said out loud.
  • The Side-by-Side Doer: closest through doing things together.
  • The Quiet Recharger: closest through calm and room to breathe.
  • The Warm Sparker: closest through warmth, play and touch.
