Privacy Policy for Vocabdrift

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Vocabdrift is a Lock Screen vocabulary app published by Baker Ventures LLC. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what that means for your data.

1. What we collect, how, and what it is used for

Nothing. Vocabdrift does not have an account system, does not send any data to a server, and does not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kit. The app makes no network requests of any kind in normal use.

2. What stays on your device

Vocabdrift stores the following locally, using Apple’s standard on-device storage APIs, solely to make the app work for you. None of it ever leaves your phone:

  • Whether you’ve completed the app’s onboarding screens.
  • Your quiz history: how many questions you’ve answered, how many you got right, and your current streak.
  • Whether you’ve turned on optional local notifications.
  • The rotation schedule the Lock Screen widget uses to show words, which is generated on your device and shared only with the widget extension via Apple’s App Group mechanism — a private channel between the app and its own widget, not a network connection.

3. Notifications

Vocabdrift can optionally schedule local notifications to remind you of new words. These are scheduled directly on your device using Apple’s notification system. No notification content is generated by or sent through a remote server — Vocabdrift has no server.

4. Who we share your data with

No one. Vocabdrift transmits nothing, so no third party — no analytics provider, no advertiser, no host, no processor — ever receives anything about you or your use of the app. Because no third party receives your data, there is none whose protection of it we would need to confirm. If that ever changes, this policy will name every recipient and state that each is contractually bound to protect your data to at least the standard set out here, and it will be updated before the change ships.

5. Data retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

  • Retention. The items in Section 2 stay on your device for as long as the app is installed. We hold no copy, so we have no retention period of our own.
  • Deletion. If you delete Vocabdrift, all of it is deleted with it. There is nothing to request the deletion of, because nothing is stored anywhere except your device.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can turn off notifications at any time in the app or in your device Settings. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no other processing by us to withdraw consent for.

6. Content and licensing

Word definitions come from WordNet® (Princeton University) and Wiktionary, both used under their respective open licenses with attribution. This is a content-licensing matter, not a data-privacy one — using the app does not share anything about you with either project.

7. Children’s privacy

Vocabdrift does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that does involve a network request — this page will be updated first, with a new “last updated” date above, before that feature ships.
