Privacy Policy for FlirtGym

Effective date: July 17, 2026
Last updated: November 3, 2026

FlirtGym is operated by Baker Ventures LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. The short version

  • Progress, histories, preferences, and saved results are stored on your device. Content you submit to AI features also goes through our server to Anthropic.
  • Sign in with Apple supplies an Apple user identifier and, when Apple provides them, your name and email (which may be Apple's private relay email). These are stored locally and sent to RevenueCat for subscription identity and support.
  • RevenueCat manages purchases. Meta/Facebook SDK and AppsFlyer measure our advertising and app events; PostHog measures product usage. The App does not display ads. Denying Apple's tracking permission does not disable all analytics or event processing.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use your submitted AI content for advertising.

2. Information we process

2.1 AI requests and reports

When you choose an AI feature, your messages, conversation context, bio/profile text, selected screenshots, or photos (including photos of your own face/body or other people in screenshots) are sent to our backend on Render and forwarded to Anthropic to generate coaching, replies, ratings, hints, analyses, or feedback. Only submit content you have the right to share; avoid sensitive information about yourself or others. Photo feedback focuses on controllable presentation, such as lighting, styling, grooming and framing. We do not use photos for facial identification.

If you report content, the report's feature/surface, character, optional details, and time are written to our hosting logs for safety review. Details may include information you choose to type.

2.2 Local storage and identity

The App stores a display name, onboarding answers, goals and optional age/state preferences, adult-age attestation, consent choices, progress, XP, streaks, settings, referral code, histories, saved AI results, subscription status, and a limited activity log locally. Looks Coaching may save small photo thumbnails locally for history. Local storage is not an exclusive-storage guarantee for content you submit to AI or for events described below.

Sign in with Apple requests name and email. Apple normally supplies these only on first authorization. The App saves available values and the Apple user identifier locally, and sends them to RevenueCat as subscriber attributes; the Apple identifier becomes the subscription app-user identifier. We do not receive your Apple password. AI requests also carry a subscription user identifier and app build number to our backend for entitlement checks, usage limits, and service protection.

2.3 Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple or Google processes store payments; we do not receive your full card number or billing address. RevenueCat receives transaction/receipt and entitlement information, a generated app-user identifier or Apple sign-in identifier, and device/technical information to verify, restore, and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat is configured when the App starts where its native SDK and key are available; it does not wait for ATT. After the ATT request resolves the App calls RevenueCat's device-identifier collection, supplies Meta's anonymous identifier, and supplies the AppsFlyer identifier when available. An available IDFA depends on iOS authorization. RevenueCat integrations can forward subscription lifecycle events to Meta's Conversions API and AppsFlyer when enabled; those server-side events are separate from client event logging. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

2.4 Permissions and device features

  • Camera and photos: requested when you take/select photos or screenshots for analysis. The App does not scan your library in the background. You can decline or revoke access in device settings.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): asks to use the advertising identifier to measure which ads brought you here. The code enables Meta advertiser tracking and advertiser-ID collection only when ATT is granted. Refusing does not stop non-IDFA events, product analytics, subscription verification, or Apple's SKAdNetwork attribution.
  • Android: configuration requests internet/network-state access for services, image-library and camera access for chosen uploads, and vibration for haptics. It explicitly blocks microphone, precise/coarse location, and legacy external-storage permissions. Final OS prompts depend on the platform/build.
  • Motion: iOS configuration includes a motion purpose string because a linked framework requires it; the source does not request motion/fitness data. The App does not request contacts, GPS location, or microphone access.
  • Copy/share actions use your device clipboard or system share sheet when you choose them. Notification preferences are stored locally; the source does not register a remote push token or integrate a push-notification SDK.

2.5 Attribution and analytics SDKs

  • Meta (Facebook) SDK: measures ad attribution and logs activation, onboarding/paywall, feature-funnel, trial and purchase events, with event properties such as product, price, currency, and static step labels. It processes a Meta anonymous identifier and technical/device/network information. Native auto-init/logging defaults are off, but the App explicitly initializes Meta and enables automatic app-event logging at launch, with advertiser flags initially off. After ATT resolves those flags follow the permission result. Thus Meta events can be processed before the ATT answer and after denial; IDFA access is permission-dependent. See Meta's privacy policy.
  • AppsFlyer: measures installs, advertising attribution, app funnel events, trials and purchases, using its install identifier, event properties, and device/network information (including IP address; available advertising identifiers depend on platform permission). The App configures manualStart at launch and explicitly starts the SDK after the ATT request resolves, whether granted or denied. Event calls also occur in the funnel; delaying SDK start is not a promise that no event can be queued before that point. The App shares its AppsFlyer identifier with RevenueCat. See AppsFlyer's services privacy policy.
  • PostHog: when configured, starts at App launch independently of ATT. It receives a random app-local anonymous identifier, manually recorded screen/feature/funnel events, subscription-result properties, build-environment labels, and SDK technical/device metadata. It does not receive AI messages, photos, or Apple name/email from our event calls. Configuration disables session replay, automatic lifecycle events, feature-flag preloading and IP geolocation, and the App does not call identify() or mount automatic touch capture. Anonymous identifiers are still identifiers, and network requests expose an IP address to the receiving service. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Apple's SKAdNetwork is also configured, with an AppsFlyer attribution-report endpoint, for privacy-preserving advertising measurement without IDFA permission. This is distinct from the SDK event flows above.

Fixed-choice onboarding quiz events include question identifiers, selected answers and step numbers, sent through the shared funnel to Meta, AppsFlyer and PostHog. PostHog practice events include scenario/category identifiers and message counts. These events do not include typed conversation text, photos, names or dates of birth from our event calls.

2.6 Technical requests and avatar images

Our backend and host process IP addresses, request metadata, app build and subscription user identifiers to route requests, verify entitlement with RevenueCat, apply usage/spend limits, and protect the service. The source maintains in-memory counters/caches; hosting and error logs may retain technical information. Upstream AI error responses may be logged in truncated form.

Most character images are bundled. When a fallback illustrated avatar is needed, the App can request it directly from DiceBear's API with a character-name seed. DiceBear receives that seed and network metadata such as IP address; this request does not send the user's profile or AI submissions. See DiceBear's privacy policy.

3. How information is used and shared

We use information to provide AI features, save local progress/history, manage and restore subscriptions, measure product usage and advertising effectiveness, respond to reports/support, and maintain reliability and service safety. Submitted AI content is not sent to our advertising/analytics SDKs by our event code.

Recipients are Anthropic for AI, Render for backend hosting and logs, Apple/Google for authentication or store billing, RevenueCat for subscription management, Meta and AppsFlyer for advertising measurement, PostHog for product analytics, and DiceBear for fallback avatars, as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

Provider policies: Anthropic, Render, Apple, Google, and the SDK policies linked above. Anthropic's commercial API content is not used for model training by default; provider retention and safety/legal exceptions are governed by its commercial data-retention policy.

4. Retention

Local histories and preferences remain until cleared by the relevant App controls or device storage removal. Uninstalling is not a request to erase vendor records, and operating-system backups or SDK-managed storage may have separate lifecycles.

Our backend does not implement a persistent database or file archive of AI submissions. It processes request content in memory. The Looks Coaching endpoint caches a derived result keyed by a hash of image bytes and rubric version, up to 500 entries, with oldest-entry eviction and no time-based expiry; images themselves are not in that cache. Entitlement, usage/spend and abuse-control records keyed by user identifier or IP are also held in memory. These caches/counters disappear on process restart or applicable eviction/reset; they are not an indefinite content archive.

Reports and operational/error records may remain in Render logs. Anthropic, hosting, store, subscription and analytics/attribution services maintain records under their own policies. We do not promise a single fixed deletion period across these systems or immediate erasure from every provider.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can choose whether to submit content, decline/revoke camera/photo or tracking permission, and turn off AI Data Sharing in Settings. Turning off AI sharing stops AI feature use; it does not turn off purchases or analytics.

Use history controls to clear the histories covered by that control. Settings → Account → Delete Account clears the App's local stored account/profile/history/progress/consent keys and signs out locally. It asks our backend to delete the corresponding RevenueCat subscriber and removes the backend entitlement cache and free-use counter for that identifier. If server deletion fails, the App reports the failure; retry behavior depends on the result. This does not automatically erase SDK-managed anonymous identifiers, previously transmitted analytics/attribution events, host logs, image-result hashes, or every service-protection counter. Contact us for help with records held by providers. You can also manage Sign in with Apple authorization in Apple settings.

Deleting the account or App does not cancel a subscription; cancel through your Apple or Google store subscription settings. Store transaction records may remain for legal/accounting obligations.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal information and to exercise rights without discrimination. Contact support@bakerventuresstudio.com. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents with a request.

6. AI, security, adults and international use

AI output may be inaccurate or unsuitable and is for practice and self-improvement, not professional advice. The App is for adults 18 and older and uses an age gate. We do not knowingly collect children's information; contact us if you believe a child has provided it.

Production service connections use HTTPS/TLS. No transmission or storage method is completely secure. We do not claim that all local data is encrypted or that a content database exists. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries with different data-protection laws.

7. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy and provide notice of material changes as appropriate. Contact Baker Ventures LLC at support@bakerventuresstudio.com with questions or privacy requests.
