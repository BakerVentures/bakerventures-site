Privacy Policy for Quietlog

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Quietlog is a perimenopause and menopause symptom-tracking app published by Baker Ventures LLC. This policy explains what the app does with what you log. It is written to match exactly what the app does, and it is kept as a standalone document because Washington’s My Health My Data Act and the FTC’s Health Breach Notification Rule both require that.

1. The short version

Quietlog doesn’t have an account, doesn’t have a server, and doesn’t send your data anywhere. Everything you log stays on your device.

2. What we collect, how, and what it is used for

Nothing is collected by us. “Collect” here means data leaving your device to reach Baker Ventures LLC or any third party. Quietlog stores what you log — symptoms, severity, an overall rating, and optional notes — in a local database on your device only, and uses it for one purpose: to show you your own history inside the app.

There is no account, no sign-in, no device identifier, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no advertising SDK in this app. The app makes no network request that carries anything you logged.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to export. The Export feature builds a CSV file, a JSON backup, and/or a clinician-ready PDF and hands it to your operating system’s own share sheet — from there, where it goes is entirely your choice (emailing it to yourself, saving it to Files, or sharing it with a clinician). Quietlog has no visibility into, and no involvement in, what you do with that exported file.

What we never do

We never sell your data — there is no data to sell, since none of it ever reaches us. We never share it with a third party. We never use it for advertising. We never predict, diagnose, or make any treatment recommendation from it; it is a tracking tool only.

3. Who we share your data with

No one. Quietlog transmits nothing, so there is no third party — no analytics vendor, no advertising network, no cloud host, no processor of any kind — that receives anything you log. Because no third party ever receives your data, there is no third party whose protection of it we would need to confirm; if that ever changes, this policy will be rewritten before that version ships, and it will name every recipient and state that each is contractually bound to protect your data to at least the standard set out here.

4. Data retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

Your data lives only on your device, so you control it directly:

  • Retention. Entries are kept on your device until you delete them. We hold no copy and therefore have no retention period of our own.
  • Deletion. Delete an entry in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. There is no account to close and no company-held record to request the deletion of, because none exists.
  • Export. Use Export at any time to take a complete copy with you before deleting anything.
  • Withdrawing consent. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no ongoing processing by us to withdraw consent for. If a future version adds an account or a sync feature, you will be asked to affirmatively opt in to whatever new data handling that feature introduces — not defaulted into it.

5. If this ever changes

If a future version adds an account, a sync feature, or any third-party SDK capable of transmitting log content, this policy will be rewritten and republished before that version ships.

6. Children

Quietlog is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
