Hinge prompts that get you messaged

Photos decide the swipe. Prompts decide whether anyone has something to open with.

Last updated 2026-08-20

A Hinge prompt succeeds when a stranger reads it and immediately has something to send you. That is the whole test. Photos decide whether someone swipes; prompts decide whether they have anything to open with. Fill all three, make them do different jobs, and be specific and slightly odd rather than broadly appealing.

The one test that matters

Read your answer and ask: what would someone message me about this? If the answer is nothing, the prompt is decoration and you have wasted one of your three slots.

"Travel, food and good vibes" gives nobody anything. "I will defend gas station coffee in front of a jury" gives everybody something. The second is not cleverer — it is just answerable.

Make the three do different jobs

  • One funny. Establishes tone and makes you a person rather than a résumé.
  • One real. Something you actually care about, said plainly. This is what makes the funny one land instead of reading as deflection.
  • One hook. An explicit invitation — a question, a claim to argue with, a detail that begs a follow-up.

Three variations on the same joke burns two slots and reads as a person with one setting.

Prompts that tend to work

PromptWhy it works
Two truths and a lieBuilt-in game — people reply just to guess

I'll fall for you ifSame, with more room to be funny
Unusual skillsSpecific by nature, hard to answer blandly
My most irrational fearSelf-deprecating and instantly relatable

Prompts and answers to avoid

Anything whose honest answer is one word. Plus the recurring offenders: jokes about hating the app, height disclaimers, "ask me anything", and answers that are a list of nouns.

The app-hating one deserves specific mention because it is extremely common and reads as resentment. She is on the app too. Opening with contempt for the thing you are both doing is a strange first impression.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best Hinge prompts for men?

The ones that give someone a specific thing to message you about. 'Two truths and a lie', 'The way to win me over is', and 'I'll fall for you if' all work well because they invite a reply. Avoid prompts whose honest answer is a single word.

How do you answer Hinge prompts?

Specifically and slightly oddly. A prompt answer succeeds when a stranger reads it and immediately has something to say. Broadly appealing answers are invisible; a strange, true detail gets messaged about far more often.

How many Hinge prompts should you fill out?

All three, and treat them as three different jobs: one funny, one that reveals something real, and one that hands over an obvious conversational hook. Three variations on the same joke wastes two of them.

What Hinge prompts should you avoid?

Anything answered in one word, anything about hating dating apps, height jokes, 'ask me anything', and answers that are just a list of nouns. All of them fail the same test: nobody can send you a message about them.

Do Hinge prompts actually matter?

They matter most for whether people message you, and least for whether they swipe. Photos decide the swipe; prompts decide whether the person who swiped has anything to open with. A profile with good photos and dead prompts gets matches and no conversations.
