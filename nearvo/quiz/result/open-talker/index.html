A connection style

The Open Talker

You feel closest when things are said out loud.

Words are how you connect. Saying how the day went, hearing “I appreciate you”, and talking things through to the end all leave you feeling near to your partner. Silence can feel like distance, even when nothing is wrong.

Worth knowing: a partner who connects in other ways may need a little time before they find their words. Pauses are not always distance.

Three things to try this week, together

  1. Set aside ten phone-free minutes after dinner and take turns telling each other about your day without interrupting.
  2. Write your partner a short note, a few honest sentences, and leave it where they will find it.
  3. Say one thing you appreciate about them every evening, out loud.

A question to ask your partner tonight

Is there something you have wanted to tell me lately but have not found the moment for?
