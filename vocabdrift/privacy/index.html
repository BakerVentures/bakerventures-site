Vocabdrift privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026  ·  Publisher: Baker Ventures LLC, Logan, Utah, USA

Vocabdrift has no account, no server, and makes no network requests in normal use. There is no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK in it. So the interesting part of this policy is not what is collected — it is nothing — but exactly where the few things it remembers are kept, and that is set out as a ledger below.

1. What we collect, how, and what it is used for

Nothing. Vocabdrift does not have an account system, does not send any data to a server, and does not include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kit. The app makes no network requests of any kind in normal use.

2. What stays on your device

Vocabdrift stores the following locally, using Apple’s standard on-device storage APIs, solely to make the app work for you. The app never sends any of it anywhere — it has no server to send it to:

The whole of it, and where each item can exist
What Vocabdrift remembersOn the handsetIn your own backupSent to anyone
Whether you have completed onboardingYesNoNo
Quiz history: questions answered, how many right, current streakYesNoNo
Whether optional local notifications are onYesNoNo
Your choices about words: favourites, words queued for review, how often you asked for a new wordYesNoNo
The rotation schedule the Lock Screen widget readsYesYesNo

The rotation schedule is generated on your device and shared only with the widget extension via Apple’s App Group mechanism — a private channel between the app and its own widget, not a network connection.

Your device’s own backup

“On your device” and “only ever on your device” are not the same thing, so this is worth being exact about. Your iPhone backup — the one you keep in iCloud or on your own computer — is a copy of your device, and it is the one place any of the above can exist outside the handset. Measured on a build of this app rather than assumed:

  • Your progress is not in your backup. Everything in that table except the widget’s rotation schedule lives in the app’s local storage directory, and iOS is told to keep that directory out of your backups; the directory carries the backup-exclusion attribute on a running build. So your quiz history, your streak, your favourited words and your notification setting stay on the handset — and because they are not in the backup, restoring to a new phone starts them fresh.
  • The widget’s word schedule is in it. The App Group file the widget reads is not excluded, so your device backup includes it like most app files. It holds the pre-computed list of upcoming words — definitions from the word list that ships inside the app, each with the date your chosen rotation puts it on. It contains nothing you typed, no quiz results and no favourites.
Both lines above were checked against a running build, not inferred from the code. The distinction matters: an app can intend to exclude a directory and fail to.

Either way, that backup is yours: it goes to your own iCloud account or your own computer, under your Apple Account. Baker Ventures cannot see it, cannot request it, and holds no copy of anything.

3. Notifications

Vocabdrift can optionally schedule local notifications to remind you of new words. These are scheduled directly on your device using Apple’s notification system. No notification content is generated by or sent through a remote server — Vocabdrift has no server.

4. Who we share your data with

No one. Vocabdrift transmits nothing, so no third party — no analytics provider, no advertiser, no host, no processor — ever receives anything about you or your use of the app. Because no third party receives your data, there is none whose protection of it we would need to confirm. If that ever changes, this policy will name every recipient and state that each is contractually bound to protect your data to at least the standard set out here, and it will be updated before the change ships.

5. Data retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

  • Retention. The items in Section 2 stay on your device for as long as the app is installed. We hold no copy, so we have no retention period of our own.
  • Deletion. Deleting Vocabdrift removes everything it stored on your device. There is nothing to request the deletion of from us, because we hold no copy. The one exception is your own backup: if a backup was taken while the app was installed, it can still hold the widget’s word schedule until you delete that backup too.
  • Withdrawing consent. You can turn off notifications at any time in the app or in your device Settings. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no other processing by us to withdraw consent for.

6. Content and licensing

Word definitions come from WordNet® (Princeton University) and Wiktionary, both used under their respective open licenses with attribution. This is a content-licensing matter, not a data-privacy one — using the app does not share anything about you with either project.

7. Children’s privacy

Vocabdrift does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that does involve a network request — this page will be updated first, with a new “last updated” date above, before that feature ships.
