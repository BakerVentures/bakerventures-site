A planning tool, not a promise

Hatch Window helps you decide when and where to go. It does not know what the fish are doing. This is the same thing the app already tells you next to every window, ranking and outlook it shows, and these terms repeat it rather than restate it differently, so the two can never drift apart:

Solunar theory (moon-based fishing windows) is a traditional angling heuristic, not a scientifically validated predictor of fish activity.

Ranking is this app's own heuristic, not a guarantee.

As a term of this agreement:

  • Solunar windows are computed astronomy, not biology. The major and minor windows come from the moon's transit, underfoot, rise and set at the coordinates you picked, with a bump where a window overlaps dawn or dusk twilight. That is all. Nothing in that calculation observes a fish, a river, a water temperature, or a season.
  • Hatch guidance is a curated chart, not a forecast. The fly suggestions are a hand-built reference matching typical insect activity to month, water type and region. Real hatches vary with the year, elevation, weather and water temperature — treat the months as typical ranges, not as a schedule.
  • Rankings and the weekly outlook are our own heuristic. "Fishing well right now" ranks spots mostly on whether recent gauge data exists and is steady. It is an ordering we invented, not a measurement of fishing quality.
  • Nothing here estimates, predicts, or guarantees a catch. You may fish a four-star window on the top-ranked river and catch nothing. That is fishing, and it is not a defect in the app.
  • Spot information is a starting point, not an authority. The curated waters and any access information are general references, not a claim that a spot is currently open, legal to fish, or safe to reach. Conditions and public access change.

Where the live numbers come from

The app fetches two public feeds directly, over HTTPS, sending only the site number or coordinates needed to answer the request:

  • USGS Water Services — streamflow and gauge height. As the app states: "Streamflow and gauge height data: USGS Water Services (waterservices.usgs.gov), provisional and subject to revision." Provisional means exactly that: the USGS may revise or withdraw these values, and they may be wrong, stale, or missing.
  • Open-Meteo — current weather. "Weather data: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)", used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

We don't control either service, don't warrant its data, and can't promise it will be available. When a feed is down, stale, or returns nothing, the app says so rather than inventing a number — but an honestly-reported gap is still a gap, and the absence of data is not a statement about the river.
