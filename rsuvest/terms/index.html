What RSUvest is

RSUvest is a personal tracking tool for equity compensation. You enter your own grant details and it computes a vesting schedule, optional value and withholding estimates, and local reminders. It is a calculator over numbers you supply — nothing more.

Not financial, tax, or investment advice

RSUvest is a personal tracking tool. All values are estimates only, not financial, tax, or investment advice — verify against your own grant documents.

Estimate only, not tax or financial advice. You enter the share price — nothing is fetched automatically.

The withholding figure is a single flat rate you choose, applied to the gross value — not real tax brackets. Actual withholding depends on your employer's payroll setup, your W-4, your state, and whether the year's supplemental-wage threshold has been crossed. None of that is knowable on this device.

Accuracy and your responsibility

Every figure the app shows is derived from what you typed in. A mistyped grant date, quantity, strike price, or share price produces a confidently wrong schedule or estimate, and the app has no way to detect that. Your grant agreement and your employer's stock plan administrator are the authoritative record; this app is not. Real vesting can also be changed by events the app knows nothing about — termination, leave, acceleration, a blackout, a stock split, or a plan amendment.
