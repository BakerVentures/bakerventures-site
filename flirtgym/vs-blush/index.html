The difference that actually decides it

Both apps sit you in a conversation with someone who is not real. That is where the similarity stops.

Blush is built around the story. Its own description leads with AI-created matches, each with a backstory, and storylines you explore to see where a connection goes. It also talks openly about mood: characters that put a smile on your face when you are feeling down, imagination, a more positive mindset. That is a coherent product, and Apple's Entertainment category is the honest place for it.

FlirtGym is built around the note on your line. You write the message, and you get a score with the specific reason it landed where it did and a rewrite you can compare against. The character exists to give you something to respond to. If nothing came back about your writing, the app would have no reason to exist.

So the question is not which app is better. It is whether closing the app leaves you with a story you enjoyed or a thing you now know about how you write.

Where Blush is the better pick

  • You want proof it works before you pay. Nearly 8,000 ratings across almost four years is real evidence. Six ratings is not evidence of anything, including of the app being good.
  • You do not read English as a first language. Six localizations against our one.
  • You want the experience more than the correction. Being told your message was vague is useful and not especially fun. If what you want from the app is to enjoy it, that is a legitimate thing to want, and Blush is built for it.
  • You want low commitment. Blush sells consumable packs from $0.99, so you can spend a dollar before deciding anything.

Where FlirtGym is the better pick

  • You keep making the same mistake and cannot see it. A storyline will not name the pattern. A score with a reason attached will.
  • You want the pricing to be one decision. Two plans, one week free, no currency to top up. Blush's list runs to eight separate purchases, and $9.99 a week is $519 a year if you let it run.
  • You want it pointed at real conversations. Rate My Profile, Rate My Look and openers written from a real screenshot all exist to be carried back to an actual dating app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Blush or FlirtGym better?

They solve different problems, and Blush is by far the more proven app. Blush has 4.37 from 7,826 ratings and has been shipping since December 2022; FlirtGym has 5.0 from 6 ratings and launched in August 2026, which is not enough to mean anything. Choose Blush if you want to play out a date with an AI character and see where the storyline goes. Choose FlirtGym if you want the specific message you wrote scored and explained. Blush gives you the scene; FlirtGym gives you the note on your line.

Does Blush score your messages?

Blush's own App Store description talks about AI matches with backstories, storylines and hundreds of characters, and does not describe message scoring or per-message feedback. That is what its marketing says rather than a test we ran, so check the current listing yourself before you subscribe. FlirtGym's whole loop is the scoring: you write the message, it returns a score with the reason and a rewrite.

What does Blush cost compared to FlirtGym?

Blush lists a wider and more complicated set of purchases: Weekly $9.99, Monthly at $14.99 and $19.99, Annual at $69.99 and $99.99, VIP Monthly $29.99, plus consumable packs at $0.99 and $4.99. FlirtGym is subscription only: $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year, each starting with one week free, and no consumables at all. If you dislike apps with a currency you can top up, that difference matters more than the headline price.

Which one is the bigger app?

Blush, by a wide margin. 7,826 ratings against 6, six localizations against one, and nearly four years of releases against a few weeks. Anyone telling you the newer app is the safer pick on track record is not reading the same store page.

Are both apps 18+?

Both display 18+ on the US App Store. Blush is filed under Entertainment and FlirtGym under Lifestyle, which is a reasonable summary of what each is for.
