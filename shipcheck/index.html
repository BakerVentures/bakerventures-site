ShipCheck

A Claude Code plugin by Baker Ventures LLC.

ShipCheck checks a React Native or Expo project for App Store and Google Play rejection risk before you submit, citing the exact guideline clause from a locally cached copy of Apple and Google policy. The scan runs on your own machine — your project never leaves it.
