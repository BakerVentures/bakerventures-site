What to say when she stops replying

One short message, no mention of the silence, and a hard stop after one unanswered follow-up. Here is the detail.

Last updated 2026-08-20

Send one short message that gives her something easy to answer, and do not mention the silence. A specific callback to something she said earlier works best — it proves you were paying attention and gives her a low-effort way back in. Skip "you there?", skip apologising for double texting, and stop after one unanswered follow-up.

Why did the conversation die?

Most dead threads die of the same cause: the last message gave her nothing to do. "How was your weekend?" gets "good." A compliment gets "thank you." A statement with no hook gets a reaction emoji and silence.

Look at your last three messages before you look at hers. If every one could be answered in a single word, the thread did not fade — you closed it. That is good news, because it is a fixable pattern rather than a verdict on you.

The other reason is simpler and worth naming: sometimes interest faded, and no message rescues that. Being able to tell the two apart is most of the skill.

What actually works as a follow-up?

  • The specific callback. "Did the sourdough survive?" beats "hey, how's it going" by an enormous margin. It is short, it is easy, and it proves you were listening.
  • The low-stakes opinion ask. Something she can answer in four words with no effort and mild amusement.
  • The direct move. If the thread was warm before it stalled, proposing something concrete is often better than another message. It gives the exchange a point.

How to stop it happening next time

The fix is upstream of the follow-up. Threads stall for structural reasons that repeat, so the durable improvement is in the messages you send while things are going fine, not the one you send after they aren't.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should you say when she stops replying?

Send one short message that gives her something easy to answer and does not mention the silence. A callback to something specific she said earlier works best. Do not send 'you there?', do not apologise for double texting, and do not send a third message if the second gets no answer.

How long should you wait before sending another message?

Long enough that you are not reacting, short enough that the thread is still warm. Two to four days is the usual sensible window. Waiting two weeks means reintroducing yourself; waiting two hours means you are anxious and it shows in what you write.

Is double texting bad?

One follow-up is normal and often works. The problem is never the second message, it is the third, and it is the content: a follow-up that comments on being ignored puts her in the position of having to explain herself, which is the least appealing thing you can ask of someone.

Why do conversations die on dating apps?

Usually because the exchange stopped giving her anything to work with. Questions that take one word to answer, compliments that need only a thank-you, and statements with no hook all end threads. Interest fading is the other reason, and it is not fixable by better texting.

How do you know when to stop?

One unanswered follow-up is information. Two is an answer. Continuing past that costs you more than the match was worth, and the time is better spent on a conversation that is actually moving.
