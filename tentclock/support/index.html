Frequently asked questions

Does TentClock need an account?
No. There's no sign-in, no account, and nothing to set up beyond picking a clock style and, if you want weather, allowing location access.
Why does TentClock ask for my location?
Only to show the current weather for where you are. If you say no, the clock and alarm still work fine — you just won't see a temperature.
Is my location sent anywhere?
Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, a weather data provider, to fetch the forecast — with no name or account attached, and nothing else. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
My alarm didn't go off. What happened?
Check that Notifications are allowed for TentClock in your device's Settings, and that the app wasn't force-closed — iOS can silence scheduled alarms for an app that was manually killed. Also check Do Not Disturb and any Focus mode.
The weather isn't showing, or looks wrong.
Confirm Location is allowed for TentClock in Settings and that you have a network connection — the app fetches the forecast live and shows nothing if it can't reach the weather service. If a reading looks off for your actual location, tell us the city and what you saw.
Does TentClock work without Wi-Fi or cell service?
The clock and alarm work fully offline. Only the weather reading needs a connection; without one it just doesn't update.
Is there anything to buy?
No. TentClock is free, with every scene and feature unlocked — no subscription, no in-app purchase, no account.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and iOS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot. That usually points us straight at it.

Price

TentClock is free, with no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no account. There’s nothing to buy, cancel, or refund.
