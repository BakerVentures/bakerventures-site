Please note. rsuvest is a tracking and planning tool. It does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice. Value and withholding figures are estimates calculated from inputs you enter yourself — including the share price — and any tax figure uses a simplified flat rate, not your real tax situation, so it is a ballpark for planning and not what you will actually owe. Your grant documents and your equity administrator are the authority on your grants; talk to a CPA or tax professional about taxes and to an advisor about decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Is rsuvest financial or tax advice?
No. It's a tracking tool. Value and tax figures are estimates you enter the inputs for — verify everything against your grant documents and a tax professional.
Where do the share prices come from?
From you. rsuvest uses no paid market-data feed. You enter the current price for value estimates, which keeps the app cheap to run and your data on your device.
How accurate is the withholding estimate?
It's a rough figure based on a rate you set, using a simplified flat withholding rate rather than your real tax situation. Real withholding depends on your employer, income, and jurisdiction — treat it as a ballpark, not a number to plan around.
What grant types are supported?
RSUs, stock options (with strike price), and ESPP, with cliffs and monthly, quarterly, annual, or custom schedules.
Does rsuvest connect to my brokerage or employer?
No. You enter grants manually; nothing is linked or synced. That's deliberate — it's how your equity data stays entirely on your phone.
Where is my equity data stored?
On your device only. No account, no cloud, no studio server — your grants, share counts, and estimates stay on your phone and we never see them. Deleting the app deletes it, so export first.
Can I track grants from more than one employer?
Yes, with Pro.
Will it remind me before a vest?
Yes — local reminders ahead of each vesting event, on your device.
Is this related to my company's fund or investor tools?
No. rsuvest is strictly personal employee-equity tracking. There's nothing investor-facing in it.
A vest date looks off around a cliff or year boundary.
rsuvest handles cliffs, partial periods, and edge dates, but grant terms vary. Check the schedule against your grant agreement, and if it still looks wrong email us the grant details (dates, shares, schedule) and we'll check the math.
How do I restore my purchase?
Your unlock is tied to the store account you bought with. Open rsuvest › Settings or the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you used originally. That should bring it back — if not, send us a screenshot of what you see and we'll dig in.
How do I cancel, or ask a billing question?
Billing runs through the store, not us, so we can't cancel or change a charge on our end. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › rsuvest. Android: Play Store › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the period ends.
How do I get a refund?
We don't process the payment — Apple handles App Store refunds — so the quickest route is reportaproblem.apple.com (Android: play.google.com/store/account): pick the purchase and choose “Request a refund.” If the store gives you any trouble, tell us and we'll help where we can.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and OS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot with anything sensitive blurred. That usually points us straight at it.

Billing, cancelling, and refunds

rsuvest sells through the App Store and Google Play, so Apple and Google — not Baker Ventures — hold the money and process refunds.
