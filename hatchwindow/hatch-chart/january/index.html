Hatches and matching patterns

Midges

Chironomidae
Typical months: January to December. Water types: Tailwater, Freestone, Spring creek. Regions: Mountain West; Northeast (Catskills/Appalachians); Midwest (Driftless/Great Lakes); South (Southern Appalachians/Southeast tailwaters).
  • Zebra Midge (nymph, size 18-22)
  • Griffith's Gnat (dry, size 18-22)
  • Rojo Midge (nymph, size 20-24)

Year-round on tailwaters, most reliable hatch on cold winter days when little else is moving.

Scuds & Sowbugs

Gammarus / Asellus spp.
Typical months: January to December. Water types: Tailwater, Spring creek. Regions: Mountain West; Northeast (Catskills/Appalachians); Midwest (Driftless/Great Lakes); South (Southern Appalachians/Southeast tailwaters).
  • Scud (olive/tan) (nymph, size 14-18)
  • Sowbug (nymph, size 14-18)

Not a hatch, a year-round staple on cold, stable tailwaters and spring creeks (the South Holston and Clinch in Tennessee, the Bighorn and San Juan out West, Pennsylvania limestoners, the Driftless Area) where crustaceans outnumber insects.
