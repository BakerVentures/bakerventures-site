This Privacy Policy explains how FlirtGym ("FlirtGym," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the FlirtGym mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

The App is operated by Baker Ventures LLC.

1. The short version

  • FlirtGym does not require an account. We do not ask for your name as an identifier, email, or phone number to use the App.
  • Most of your data — your progress, stats, streaks, quiz history, and settings — is stored only on your device. We never receive the stored values themselves.
  • The App does send usage analytics and ad-attribution events to three third-party services (Meta, AppsFlyer and PostHog): which screens and features you use, your subscription plan and price, and the fixed-choice answers from the onboarding quiz. Never your name, photos, screenshots or message text. Section 2.5a lists all three and exactly what each receives.
  • When you use AI features (practice conversations, profile and photo analysis — including photos of yourself in Rate My Look — and the other coaching tools), the text, screenshots, and photos you submit are sent to our server and then to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate a response. We do not use this content to build a profile of you or sell it.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your credit card or full payment details.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you actively submit for AI processing

To provide the App's AI-powered features, the content you choose to submit is transmitted to our backend server and forwarded to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to generate a result. Depending on the feature you use, this content includes:

  • Practice conversations — the messages you type in roleplay practice sessions, so the AI character can respond and so we can generate ratings, hints, and end-of-session feedback.
  • Coaching and "Ask the Coach" messages — questions and chat text you send to get advice.
  • Photos of yourself — for Rate My Look, you take or upload one or more photos of yourself (which may show your face and body) so the AI can score and give feedback on controllable aspects of your presentation, such as lighting, styling, grooming, and framing. For Photo Ranker and Looks Coaching, you upload photos of yourself; for Rate My Profile, you upload screenshots of your own dating profile (which typically include photos of yourself).
  • Screenshots of profiles and conversations — for Decode Her Profile, Reply to Her Text, Conversation Rescue, and the Profile Analyzer, you upload screenshots that may contain another person's profile, photos, or messages, so the AI can analyze them and suggest a response.
  • Your own message and profile text — for Rate My Text and the Bio Reviewer, you submit message drafts, bios, or profile text for review.

Important — photos of yourself (Rate My Look and related features): Some features ask you to provide photos of your own face and body. When you do, those images are sent to our backend server and on to Anthropic to generate your feedback, the same way as any other content you submit. We do not use them to identify you, to build a profile of you, or for any purpose other than returning your result, and they are not retained by us in a database (see Sections 4 and 6). Providing these photos is entirely optional — the feature only runs when you choose to add a photo.

This content is processed to produce your result and is not used to identify you. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information (your own or others') that you do not want processed by an AI service. You are responsible for the content you choose to submit, including screenshots that may contain another person's information.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your device (using the operating system's standard local storage). The stored values are not transmitted to our backend server. Where an item is also reported to our analytics or attribution providers as an event property, it says so and Section 2.5a has the detail:

  • A display name you optionally enter during setup. This stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Your onboarding quiz answers. These stay on your device, and the fixed-choice answers are also sent as analytics event properties to Meta, AppsFlyer and PostHog (see Section 2.5a).
  • Your goals, and optional age range and U.S. state preferences.
  • Your progress and gameplay data: streaks, session counts, Rizz Scores, XP, levels, session history, study/quiz history, and daily missions. These values stay on your device. When you finish a practice session the App does report which scenario it was and how many messages were exchanged as an analytics event — never the conversation itself.
  • App settings: selected theme, "brutal mode," notification preferences, and a locally generated referral code.
  • Subscription status flags used to unlock paid features on your device.
  • A local, on-device activity log used for debugging, capped to the most recent entries.
  • For Looks Coaching, a small thumbnail image of each analyzed photo is saved on your device so you can see your history over time (capped to your most recent checks). These thumbnails are stored only on your device, are never uploaded to us, and you can delete them at any time using Clear history on the Looks History screen.

Because this data lives on your device, deleting the App removes it. You can also clear your Looks history from within the App at any time.

2.3 Purchase information

If you subscribe, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store and (where applicable) our subscription-management provider, RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of subscription status but do not receive your payment card number or billing address.

2.4 Device permissions

  • Camera and Photo Library — requested so you can take or select photos and screenshots for the App's photo-based features, including Rate My Look (photos of yourself), Photo Ranker, Looks Coaching, Rate My Profile, the Profile Analyzer, and the screenshot-based Wingman tools. The App accesses your camera or photo library only when you actively take or choose a photo for one of these features; it does not scan your library in the background.

2.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect or request: account credentials, email address, phone number, contacts, precise GPS location, health data, motion or fitness data, or browsing history. The App shows no advertising of any kind and contains no ad-display or ad-mediation code.

2.5a Attribution and analytics SDKs

The App includes three third-party SDKs that receive data about how the App is used. We list them, and what each one receives, in full:

  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — advertising attribution, so we can tell which of our own ads brought someone to the App. It starts when the App launches with advertiser-ID collection and advertiser tracking switched off, and from that point it reports app events (such as install, onboarding and paywall steps, trial start, and purchase) to Meta without your advertising identifier. Your device advertising identifier (IDFA) is accessed only if you allow tracking at the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • AppsFlyer — install and advertising attribution. It is configured at launch but deliberately not started until after you have answered the App Tracking Transparency prompt (whether you allow or deny it). It then receives install and in-app events, including onboarding and paywall steps, trial start, and subscription amount and currency. It receives your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. The App also participates in Apple's SKAdNetwork, Apple's privacy-preserving ad-attribution system.
  • PostHog — our own product analytics, so we can see which screens and features people use and where they drop off. It is deliberately identifier-free: no advertising identifier, no automatic screen or touch capture, no session recording, no IP-based location lookup, and no user profile. Events are tied only to a random identifier generated on your device for this App alone. It is not used for advertising and needs no tracking permission.

What these events contain. Event properties are short, fixed labels: the screen or feature used, which practice scenario and how many messages were exchanged in it, the subscription plan, price, currency and App Store country, and the fixed-choice answers you give in the onboarding quiz (for example your main goal and where you struggle most). They do not contain your name, your date of birth, your email, your photos, your screenshots, or the text of your messages, practice conversations, or bios.

The App declares to Apple that it carries out tracking, and it presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt for the reasons above. The three SDKs listed here measure our advertising elsewhere; they do not place advertising inside the App.

2.6 Automatic technical processing (IP address)

When your device contacts our backend to use an AI feature, our server and hosting provider transiently process your device's IP address to route the request and to enforce rate limits that protect the service against abuse and excessive use. We do not use IP addresses to identify you or build a profile of you, and we do not retain them for that purpose.

We use the information described above to:

  • Save your progress, stats, and preferences on your device.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the content you submit to serve advertising.

4. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the App:

  • Anthropic (AI provider). Content you submit for AI features (messages, screenshots, bios) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. Anthropic processes this data to return a result. Per Anthropic's commercial API terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy practices at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Apple. Handles in-app purchases and subscription billing.
  • RevenueCat. Manages and verifies subscription entitlements, and forwards subscription events (such as trial start and purchase) to Meta's Conversions API and to AppsFlyer for advertising-attribution measurement. To let it match those events, the App passes RevenueCat the anonymous identifiers Meta and AppsFlyer generate on your device, and your advertising identifier where you have allowed tracking.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook). We use the Meta SDK to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. It reports app events (such as install, onboarding and paywall steps, trial start and purchase) to Meta for ad attribution. With your permission (via the App Tracking Transparency prompt) it may also collect your device advertising identifier (IDFA); if you deny tracking, those events are reported without it and we rely on non-IDFA measurement. See Meta's data policy at https://www.facebook.com/policy.php.
  • AppsFlyer. Measures which ad brought you to the App. Receives install and in-app event data — onboarding and paywall steps, trial start, and subscription amount and currency — and is started only after the App Tracking Transparency prompt is answered. It receives your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. See https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/.
  • PostHog. Our product-analytics provider. Receives identifier-free events about screen and feature usage, practice sessions, and the purchase funnel, tied only to a random identifier generated on your device for this App alone. No advertising identifier, no session recording, no IP-based location. Not used for advertising. See https://posthog.com/privacy.
  • Our hosting provider. Our backend runs on a third-party cloud host that processes requests in transit. Our backend acts as a stateless relay: it forwards your request to the AI provider and returns the response, and is not designed to store the content of your conversations, screenshots, or bios in a database.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

Each third party named above — including our analytics tools, advertising-attribution providers and the third-party SDKs in the App — is engaged under terms that require them to protect user data to the same or an equal standard as this policy describes, and to use the data only for the purposes set out here. We do not sell personal information to any of them, and we do not authorise any of them to use data from the App to build an advertising profile of you. We have no parent, subsidiary or other related entity with access to user data.

5. AI processing — important disclosures

The App uses generative AI. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable, and is provided for entertainment and self-improvement practice only — it is not professional, psychological, legal, or relationship advice. Do not rely on it as such. When you upload a screenshot of another person's profile, you are responsible for ensuring you have any rights necessary to do so.

6. Data retention

  • On-device data is retained until you delete it within the App (for example, by resetting progress) or uninstall the App.
  • AI request content is transmitted to generate your result and is not retained by us in a database. Our AI and hosting providers may retain data transiently for processing, abuse-prevention, and as required by their own policies and applicable law.
  • Analytics and attribution events (Section 2.5a) are retained by Meta, AppsFlyer and PostHog under their own retention policies, which we do not control. We do not combine them with the content you submit for AI processing, and we do not use them to build an advertising profile of you.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the App, our server, and our providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can also permanently delete your account and associated subscriber record at any time in the App at Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Permissions. You can revoke camera and photo access anytime in your device Settings (the related features will stop working).
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Regional rights. Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
