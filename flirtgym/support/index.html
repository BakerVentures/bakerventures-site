FlirtGym support

A real person reads this inbox. Almost every message is about money, and almost every answer is “Apple, not us” — so that part is first, with the exact tap path.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Email support@bakerventuresstudio.com. One person answers it, usually within a day or two. Billing, cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us, and the steps for each are below.

MoneyBilling, cancelling and refunds

FlirtGym subscriptions are sold by Apple. That means Apple takes the payment, Apple issues the refunds, and we never see your card. It also means the cancel button is in iOS Settings, not in the app.

The usual fiveQuestions that arrive every week

How do I cancel FlirtGym Pro?
Apple handles the billing, so cancelling happens in Apple’s settings, not in the app. On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name at the top, Subscriptions, FlirtGym, Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to stop the next charge. If you are inside a free trial, cancelling before it ends means you are not charged, and you keep access until the trial period runs out. FlirtGym cannot cancel a subscription for you and cannot see your payment details.
I paid but the app still shows me the paywall.
Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. That asks Apple for your purchase history and re-applies it. If it still does not unlock, check that you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, since a family member’s account or a second Apple ID is the usual cause. If Restore fails on the right account, email support@bakerventuresstudio.com with the date of purchase and we will sort it out.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us, because Apple is the merchant of record. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, find FlirtGym and choose Request a Refund. We cannot approve or deny a refund and we do not see the outcome. If Apple declines and you think that is wrong, email us and we will tell you honestly whether we think you have a case.
Is there a free trial?
Where offered. Apple decides whether a free trial is shown to you, based on your eligibility, and the subscription screen displays one only if you qualify. If you do not see a trial there, the price shown is what you pay. There is no free tier: practice, scoring and coaching are all part of Pro.
How do I delete my account and my data?
In the app: Settings, Account, Delete Account. It is a real deletion, not a deactivation. It removes your account and the associated subscriber record, and signs you out of Apple and RevenueCat. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription, because Apple owns that relationship, so cancel first using the steps above or you will keep being billed for an account that no longer exists.

PrivacyWhat the app can and cannot see

Does FlirtGym read my real conversations?
Only what you paste or screenshot into it, and only to produce the reply or the score you asked for. It has no access to your Messages, your dating apps or your camera roll beyond the image you choose. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
Where does my practice text actually go?
To our server and on to Anthropic, which makes the model, purely to generate the response or the score you asked for. Under Anthropic’s commercial API terms those inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. Our backend is a relay and is not designed to store the content of your conversations, screenshots or bios in a database.
Why does FlirtGym ask permission to track me?
Because it honestly carries out tracking as Apple defines it, and declares so. The app includes three third-party kits — Meta and AppsFlyer, which measure whether our own advertising elsewhere worked, and PostHog, our own product analytics. If you deny the prompt, none of them receive your advertising identifier and the app works exactly the same. What they receive in either case is short fixed labels: which screen or feature you used, which practice scenario and how many messages were exchanged, your plan, price and store country, and your fixed-choice onboarding answers. Never your photos, your screenshots or the text of anything you wrote.
Are my progress and scores stored on your servers?
No. Your streaks, scores, levels, session history, settings and any display name you entered are held on your device, so deleting the app removes them. There is no account required to use FlirtGym at all.

PlatformsWhat runs where

Is FlirtGym on Android or iPad?
No. iPhone only, iOS 16.4 or later. There is no Android version and no iPad version, and we would rather say that plainly than leave you waiting for one. The browser tools below work on any device, including Android.
Something is broken, or a score looks wrong.
Email support@bakerventuresstudio.com. Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened, and include your iOS version if you can find it. A screenshot helps more than a description. We read every one of these.
Can I suggest a scenario or a character?
Yes, and those are the most useful messages we get. Describe the situation you cannot get through in real life — that is the thing worth building a practice scenario around.
