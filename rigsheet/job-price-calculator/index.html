How the price is built

Labor is hours on site times your rate, plus drive time at the same rate if the box is ticked. Mileage is miles times your cost per mile. Overhead is monthly overhead divided by jobs per month. Add materials and you have your cost before fees.

The card fee is a percentage of the price, so it cannot simply be added. The price solves price = (cost + fixed fee) / (1 - fee% - margin%), which means the fee and your margin are both taken out of the final number, not out of your cost.

Two reference points, both from trade guides rather than from any primary survey, and both checked on 2026-10-13. Jobber's detailing pricing guide (originally published October 2023, last updated 16 April 2026) says "hourly rates generally fall between $50 and $100/hour". InvoiceFly's mobile detailing prices (published 27 May 2025, updated 9 July 2026) says "mobile services typically charge 15-20% more than fixed location detailers due to travel expenses", and elsewhere on the same page puts the premium at 15 to 25 percent. These are two companies selling software to detailers, not a standard anyone is bound by, and there is no primary source for either figure that I could find. Check your own local competitors before you trust any of them, including this page.
