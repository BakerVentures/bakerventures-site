Security Deposit Return Deadline Calculator

Pick the state and the date you moved out. You get the date the statute puts the landlord's deadline on, the section it comes from, and the condition that can change it.

Information, not legal advice. This page reports what eight state statutes say, read from the statute text on 2026-10-13. It does not know your lease, your city or county rules, or your facts, and it does not say who wins a dispute. Statutes change. Read the linked text or ask a lawyer licensed in your state before you rely on a date.

What this does not cover

  • Other states. California, Georgia and Utah are not here yet, and neither are the other 42 states and DC. The MoveWitness app carries entries for California, Georgia and Utah; they are not in this calculator because I could not read their official statute text on the day I built it.
  • Weekends and holidays. The sections quoted below count calendar days and say nothing about rolling a deadline off a weekend, so this page does not roll it either. Court rules on counting time may differ.
  • Local law. Some cities and counties add their own deposit rules on top of state law.
  • What a landlord may deduct, and what happens if the deadline is missed. Those are separate questions with their own sections, and this page does not answer them.

Sources, all read 2026-10-13

  • Florida: Fla. Stat. 83.49(3)(a), 2026 edition, flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/83.49.
  • Illinois: 765 ILCS 710/1(a), Security Deposit Return Act, as amended by P.A. 103-224 eff. 1-1-24, ilga.gov.
  • New York: N.Y. General Obligations Law 7-108(1-a)(e), nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GOB/7-108.
  • North Carolina: N.C. Gen. Stat. 42-52, ncleg.gov.
  • Ohio: Ohio Rev. Code 5321.16(B), (C), codes.ohio.gov.
  • Pennsylvania: 68 P.S. 250.512(a), (e), Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951, legis.state.pa.us.
  • Texas: Tex. Prop. Code 92.103(a), 92.107(a). The official site, statutes.capitol.texas.gov, renders in the browser and could not be read by a script, so I read the section text on the mirror texas.public.law. Check the official page.
  • Washington: RCW 59.18.280(1)(a), app.leg.wa.gov.
