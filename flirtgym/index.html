How does FlirtGym work?

You pick a scenario, and an AI character plays the other side of the conversation. You write the messages yourself. When the session ends, every message you sent comes back scored, with a specific note on what landed and what to change.

Three things make the practice realistic rather than decorative. Characters have their own personalities and respond differently to the same line. They disengage or end the chat when your effort drops, so a practice conversation can be lost the way a real one can. And a private Coach sits inside the session: ask for a hint or the exact line to send, and the character never knows you asked.

Outside of practice sessions, the Wingman tools work on your real conversations. Paste a screenshot of a profile and get an opener. Paste a stalled thread and get a reply that restarts it.

What makes FlirtGym different from other rizz apps?

Other apps write the line for you. FlirtGym scores the line you wrote. That difference determines what you leave with: a message to send tonight, or a skill that works the next time the app is not open.

Who is FlirtGym for?

FlirtGym is for adults who freeze on openers, overthink every text, and watch good matches go quiet. If the gap between what you meant to say and what you actually sent is the problem, this is built for that gap.

It is not a dating app. There is no feed, no matches, no other users, no audience, and nothing you do is visible to anyone. The point of practicing somewhere private is that you can be bad at it first.

It is also not a confidence-affirmation app. Scores are honest, characters disengage when you coast, and profile feedback tells you what is not working. That is the value: finding out here rather than there.
