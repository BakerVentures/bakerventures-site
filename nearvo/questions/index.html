25 questions to ask your partner

Pick one tonight, ask it over dinner or on a walk, and then listen. Five groups, five questions each, all original.

  • Notice and remember
  • Say it out loud
  • Do something together
  • Room to breathe
  • Warmth and play

For fun and reflection. These are conversation starters, not therapy, counseling or a diagnosis. There are no right answers.

Notice and remember

For the partner who feels closest when the small things are seen.

  1. What is a small thing I do that you hope I never stop doing?
  2. What did you notice about me this week that I may not know you noticed?
  3. What is a detail about your day I could remember to ask about next time?
  4. What is a tiny gesture from me that made you feel seen recently?
  5. What is something you wish I would pay a little more attention to?

Say it out loud

For the partner who feels closest when things are put into words.

  1. What is something you appreciate about me that you do not say often?
  2. When did you last feel really listened to by me, and what helped?
  3. Is there something on your mind you have been waiting for the right moment to share?
  4. What is one thing I could say to you more often?
  5. How do you like me to start a hard conversation?

Do something together

For the partner who feels closest when you are building or going somewhere side by side.

  1. What is a small project we could take on together this month?
  2. Which of our regular routines do you enjoy most, and which would you change?
  3. What is a chore you would happily share with me?
  4. Where would you like us to go on a short outing in the next few weeks?
  5. What is something we always say we will do and never do?

Room to breathe

For the partner who feels closest after calm and a little space.

  1. What does a good, quiet evening look like for you?
  2. How can I tell you need some space without it feeling like a rejection?
  3. What helps you feel ready to reconnect after time alone?
  4. What is one demand on your time you would like us to lighten?
  5. What is the best time of day for us to talk?

Warmth and play

For the partner who feels closest through laughter, touch and a good mood.

  1. What is something silly we could do tonight just to laugh?
  2. What kind of touch feels best to you after a long day?
  3. What is a memory of us laughing that still makes you smile?
  4. How would you like me to greet you when we see each other after work?
  5. What is something playful we have not done in a while?
