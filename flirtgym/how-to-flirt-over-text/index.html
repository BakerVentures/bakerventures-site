How to flirt over text

Subtext over statements, playful over complimentary, and never escalate past what she is giving back.

Last updated 2026-08-20

Flirting over text works on subtext, not statements. A message that is funny on the surface and interested underneath lets her choose which layer to answer — that choice is what makes flirting comfortable rather than pressuring. Stay playful rather than complimentary, be specific rather than generic, and never escalate past what she is giving back.

The rule that covers most of it

Match, then nudge. Read the energy she is putting in, meet it, and go slightly further only when she goes further first. Almost everything that reads as creepy is a violation of that ordering: escalation she has not matched.

This is also why identical messages land completely differently with different people. There is no line that works in isolation. There is only a line that fits or does not fit where the conversation currently is.

Playful beats complimentary

A compliment asks for a thank-you. A tease asks for a response. That is the whole difference, and it is why teasing sustains a conversation while complimenting ends one.

Reading whether it is landing

  • She adds material instead of answering the question and stopping.
  • She teases back. The clearest signal there is.
  • She asks you things. Curiosity is hard to fake and easy to notice.
  • Length and energy roughly match yours. A widening gap is information.

Polite one-word replies are an answer. Reading that early saves everyone time, and noticing it is a skill worth practising deliberately.

Where practice helps most

Calibration is the hard part, and it is the part you cannot learn from a list of lines. Knowing that a message is too much for this moment requires having been wrong about it before.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you flirt over text without being creepy?

Keep it playful rather than complimentary, specific rather than generic, and calibrated to what she is giving back. Flirting reads as creepy mainly when it escalates past where the other person is, or when it comments on her body rather than on her. Match her level and let her set the pace.

What makes flirting work over text?

Subtext. A message that is funny on the surface and interested underneath gives her a choice about which layer to answer, which is what makes flirting comfortable. Stating interest flatly removes that choice and puts her on the spot.

How do you know if she is flirting back?

She adds material rather than just answering, she teases you, she asks questions of her own, and her messages roughly match yours in length and energy. One-word replies are an answer even when they are polite.

Should you use emojis?

Sparingly, and to soften tone rather than to carry it. An emoji is useful for signalling that a teasing line is a joke. A message made mostly of emojis reads as effort avoidance.

What should you avoid?

Compliments about her body, escalation she has not matched, anything that requires her to reassure you, and lines you obviously copied. All four fail for the same reason: they make her responsible for managing your side of the interaction.
