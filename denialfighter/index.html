Got a denial? Write the appeal you didn't know you could.

Denial Fighter helps you understand a claim denial and build an organized, personalized appeal letter — with the deadlines and documents you need to send it.

Denial Fighter · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

Understand the denial in plain language.Enter what your insurer said and Denial Fighter helps you make sense of the reason code and what an appeal actually needs to address.
A structured appeal letter, drafted with you.Answer a few guided questions and the app assembles a clear appeal letter you can review, edit, and send under your own name.
Deadlines you won't blow.Track your appeal window — internal appeal and, where applicable, external review — so your letter goes out in time to count.
Keep the paper trail together.Store the denial, your letter, and supporting documents for one claim in one place, so a follow-up call isn't a scramble.
Know your next step.If a first-level appeal is denied, Denial Fighter points you toward the next option — a second-level or external/independent review.

How it works

  1. Enter the denial — insurer, claim, and the reason given.
  2. Answer the guided questions and let the app draft your appeal letter.
  3. Review, edit, and send it yourself, then track the deadline for a response.

Questions

Does Denial Fighter guarantee my claim gets approved?
No. It helps you build a stronger, on-time appeal, but the insurer or reviewer decides the outcome. No app can promise approval.
Is this legal or medical advice?
No. Denial Fighter is a self-help drafting and organizing tool, not a lawyer, doctor, or insurance agent. For complex or high-dollar denials, consider professional help.
Does it submit the appeal for me?
No. You send your appeal yourself, so it goes on the record under your name with your signature and any documents you choose to include.
Will it contact my insurance company?
No. The app never talks to your insurer on its own; it prepares what you send.
Is my health information private?
Everything is stored on your device — no account, no studio server. Your claim details and health information stay on your phone. Review the privacy policy before entering sensitive details.
What kinds of denials does it help with?
Common denials such as “not medically necessary,” out-of-network, coding, and prior-authorization issues — the app helps you frame the appeal to the reason given.
Please note. Denial Fighter is a self-help drafting and organizing tool — not a lawyer, doctor, insurer, or insurance agent — and nothing on this page or in the app is legal, medical, or insurance advice. No app can promise a claim will be approved. The controlling facts are on your own denial letter and in your plan documents; they can set different or shorter deadlines than any general figure the app shows. Confirm your deadline and appeal steps with your plan or a patient advocate before you rely on the app. For complex or high-dollar denials, consider professional help.
