The FlirtGym free trial

One week free on both Pro plans, and the cancel steps on the same page as the offer.

Last updated 2026-09-04

FlirtGym Pro starts with one week free, on the monthly plan and on the annual plan. It converts to the paid plan at the end of the week unless you cancel first, at $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year. There is also a free tier with no time limit, which is a different thing and is explained below.

Start the free week

Free to download. FlirtGym Pro starts with one week free, then $14.99/mo or $59.99/yr. Cancel in Apple Account settings.

What you actually get for the week

Everything. The trial is not a reduced version of Pro, it is Pro.

  • Unlimited practice conversations with AI characters, across texting, dating apps and in-person scenarios
  • Every message you write scored, with the specific reason it landed where it did
  • The in-session Coach, which gives you a hint or the exact line, privately
  • Rate My Profile and Rate My Look
  • Every scenario, including cold approach
  • The full lesson and challenge track

How to cancel before you are charged

Apple handles the billing, so this happens in iOS Settings and not in the app. It takes under a minute.

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap FlirtGym
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

Do it at least 24 hours before the week is up. You keep access for the rest of the trial either way, so cancelling on day one does not end your week on day one. If something goes wrong, support is one inbox and a person reads it.

Who is eligible

Apple allows one introductory offer per person per subscription group. If you have never had a FlirtGym trial, you get the week. If you have already used one, you are charged from the start, and the app shows you the plain price instead of trial copy rather than advertising an offer the store will not honour.

The current offer is live in 175 countries and regions, verified 2026-09-04.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlirtGym have a free trial?

Yes. Both FlirtGym Pro plans, monthly and annual, start with one week free. The current offer applies in 175 countries and regions. FlirtGym has offered a trial since launch; what changed on 2026-09-04 is the length, from three days to a week. Apple allows one introductory offer per person per subscription group, so it is available if you have not already used a FlirtGym trial. If you have, the app shows you the plain price rather than trial copy, because advertising an offer the store will not honour is worse than showing the real number.

How long is the FlirtGym free trial?

One week, on every plan. A weekly plan has been submitted to Apple and is not yet purchasable; when it goes live it starts with the same free week. A week is deliberate: the app is built on repetition, and three days is not enough repetition to tell you whether it works for you.

How do I cancel the free trial before I am charged?

On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name at the top, Subscriptions, FlirtGym, Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before the week ends. You keep Pro access until the trial period finishes, so cancelling on day one does not cut you off on day one. FlirtGym cannot cancel it for you, because Apple owns the billing relationship.

What happens when the free week ends?

It converts to the paid plan automatically at $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year, charged to your Apple Account, unless you cancelled first. That is how every introductory offer on the App Store works, and it is why the cancel steps are on this page rather than buried.

Do I need the trial to use FlirtGym at all?

No, and this is the part most apps do not have. There is a free tier with no time limit on it: practice conversations, message scoring and coaching are all available at a daily allowance, for as long as you want, without paying and without starting a trial. The trial lifts the daily cap. The free tier is not a countdown.

Is there a FlirtGym free trial without a credit card?

The trial itself goes through your Apple Account, so it uses whatever payment method is already on file there, the same as any App Store subscription. If you would rather not start a subscription at all, use the free tier, or use the browser tools on this site, which need no account and no payment method of any kind.
