Is this conversation one-sided?

Paste a thread. It measures who is actually carrying it: share of messages, relative length, who asks the questions, and who restarts it.

Free, no account. Runs entirely in your browser, so the conversation is never sent anywhere.

What the text shows
    What it does not show

    Text measures reciprocity, not feeling. Someone can be busy, bad at texting, or in a different timezone, and none of that appears here. Treat this as the shape of the conversation, not a verdict on the person.

    Worth trying

    Why reciprocity and not sentiment

    Plenty of tools claim to read feeling out of a text message. They are guessing, and so is anyone who tells you a message "means" something. What text genuinely contains is effort, and how it is distributed.

    Five things are actually measurable in a thread, and they are the five this checks:

    SignalWhat it means
    Share of messagesWho is sending more of them
    Relative lengthWhether one side writes paragraphs at one-liners
    Question splitWho is doing the work of continuing it
    One-word reply rateHow often a reply closes rather than opens
    Who restarts itWho comes back after a gap

    Any one of these alone is weak evidence. A short reply is just a short reply. It is the combination that shows a conversation being carried rather than shared.

    Score a single message instead

    This looks at a whole thread. If you want one message graded, the opener score tool scores an opener out of 10 against a study of 80, and the flirting style quiz shows the default you bring to every conversation.

    Practice the harder version

    Reading a dead thread is easier than saving one. FlirtGym scores the messages you write inside a live practice conversation with an AI match, so you find out mid-conversation instead of afterwards.

    Questions

    Can this tell me if she is losing interest?

    No. It can tell you whether the effort in a thread is balanced, which is a different and more useful thing. Anything claiming to read feeling from text is guessing.

    Is anything sent to a server?

    No. It is JavaScript running on your device. Nothing you paste is transmitted, logged or stored, which matters more here than on the other tools because you are pasting someone else's messages.

    How many messages does it need?

    At least four, and it is more meaningful past ten. Below that, one short reply skews everything.
