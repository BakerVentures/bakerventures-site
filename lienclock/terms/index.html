What lienclock is

lienclock is an informational deadline tracker for mechanics lien statutes. You enter a state, your role, and the dates for a job; it counts the statutory deadlines that follow and shows you the section it read them from. It is a reference and a calendar, not a representative.

Not legal advice

This app reports what the statute says and cites the section it read. It is not legal advice, it does not file anything for you, and it does not tell you what to do.

Statutes change, and courts reinterpret them. Every deadline in this app comes from a statute read on the date shown next to its citation — that snapshot can go out of date. Missing a mechanics lien deadline can extinguish your claim permanently, so confirm any deadline you are relying on with an attorney licensed in that state before you act on it.

Baker Ventures LLC is not a law firm, no one here is your attorney, and using this app creates no attorney-client relationship and no confidentiality. Nothing the app displays is a recommendation about what you should do on your job.

Coverage has limits, and the app says so

Each state's rule set lists what it does not cover, and you should read that list for any state you rely on. Mechanics lien law also turns on facts this app never asks for — the terms of your contract, whether the project is public or private, bonding, notice actually given, and more. A deadline the app does not show you may still exist.

Generated documents

This app generates this text. It does not file, mail, or record it, and it is not legal advice.

Where a statute prescribes a form, the app reproduces it and fills in the facts you entered. It deliberately leaves party names, property descriptions, and amounts blank rather than guessing them — completing, signing, notarizing where required, serving, and recording are all yours to do, on time and correctly.
