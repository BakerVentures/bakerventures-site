Frequently asked questions

The widget isn't in the lock-screen picker.
This is a known iOS quirk with newly installed apps — restart your phone once and Vocabdrift will appear under Customize. It's not something you did wrong.
How do I add the widget?
Press and hold your lock screen, tap Customize, tap the row below the time, and pick Vocabdrift. The app's onboarding walks you through it with pictures.
How often does the word change?
Vocabdrift rotates on a schedule you set. iOS refreshes lock-screen widgets on its own timing, so we load a long run of upcoming words in advance — the widget plays them back without needing to fetch anything.
Why isn't the word updating exactly when I expect?
iOS controls when lock-screen widgets refresh and limits how often. Vocabdrift precomputes the schedule so it stays close, but the system has the final say on timing.
Does it need internet?
No. Your words are stored on your device and rotate offline. There's no account and no server call to see them.
Why did a word I already knew come back?
That's the method working, not a bug. Vocabdrift uses spaced repetition, which brings a word back right before you'd likely forget it — so words resurface at growing intervals even once they feel easy. Each time you mark one correct, the gap before you see it again gets longer. It feels repetitive early on, then thins out fast as words stick.
What's the quiz for?
Glancing at a word helps you recognize it; the active-recall quiz makes you retrieve it, which is what actually moves it into memory.
Can I choose the topic?
Yes — pick from packs like SAT, GRE, TOEFL, and everyday vocabulary. Pro unlocks all of them.
Can I control how many new words a day?
With Pro, yes — set the rotation pace from a gentle one a day up to a fast pace.
How do I review words I keep missing?
Pro tracks your quiz results and lets you favorite and re-drill the ones you miss most.
A definition or word is wrong.
Thanks for catching it — we'd rather know. Definitions come from a curated set, and mistakes do slip through. Tell us the exact word and what looks off, and we'll verify it and fix it in an update.
Where's my progress stored?
All on your device. Vocabdrift has no account and no studio server, so your progress and mastered words stay on your phone and never come to us. They're in your iCloud or Google backup if that's enabled, and deleting the app clears them.
Does my progress sync across devices?
Not directly — because Vocabdrift keeps everything on-device with no account, progress lives on the phone you study on rather than syncing between devices. The upside is total privacy; the tradeoff is no cross-device sync for now. If your devices share an iCloud or Google backup, a restore carries data over, but it won't sync live between them.
How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Open Vocabdrift → Settings or the paywall → Restore Purchases, signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, and it should come back. If not, send us a screenshot and we'll help.
How do I cancel, or ask a billing question?
Billing runs through the store, not us. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vocabdrift. Android: Play Store → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period ends.
How do I get a refund?
We don't process the charge — Apple handles App Store refunds — so the fastest route is reportaproblem.apple.com (Android: play.google.com/store/account): pick the purchase and choose “Request a refund.” If Apple gives you any trouble, let us know and we'll help.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) device and OS version, (2) what you were doing (a review, a quiz, adding words…), (3) expected vs. actual, and (4) a screenshot.

Billing, cancelling, and refunds

Vocabdrift sells through the App Store and Google Play, so Apple and Google — not Baker Ventures — hold the money and process refunds.
