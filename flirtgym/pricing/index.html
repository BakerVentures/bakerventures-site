FlirtGym pricing

What the free tier gets you, what Pro costs, why there is no trial, and how the price compares to the rest of the category.

Last updated 2026-08-20

FlirtGym is free to download. FlirtGym Pro is $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year. There is no free trial — the free tier is the trial, capped by daily usage rather than by locked features. Subscriptions are billed by Apple and cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Why is there no free trial?

Because a free tier is a better version of the same idea. A three-day trial pressures you to evaluate a practice tool in three days, which is exactly the wrong timescale for something whose whole premise is repetition.

The free tier has no clock on it. You can practice at the daily limit indefinitely and never pay. The upgrade makes sense at the point where the limit — not a countdown — is the thing in your way. That is a cleaner signal for you and an honest one for us.

How does this compare to the rest of the category?

FlirtGym's annual plan is toward the cheaper end; its monthly plan is about average. Prices in this category range widely, and the weekly plans are the ones worth reading carefully.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does FlirtGym cost?

FlirtGym is free to download and use at a limited daily allowance. FlirtGym Pro is $14.99 per month or $59.99 per year, which works out to about $5 per month on the annual plan. There is no free trial.

Is there a FlirtGym free trial?

No. FlirtGym removed its free trial in 2026. The free tier is the trial: you can use the app at a daily limit without paying, for as long as you want, and upgrade when the limit becomes the thing stopping you.

What do you get with FlirtGym Pro?

Unlimited practice sessions, every scenario including cold approach, all coaching tools, Rate My Profile and Rate My Look, the in-session Coach, and the full lesson and challenge track. The free tier caps how much of this you can use per day rather than locking features away permanently.

How do I cancel FlirtGym Pro?

Cancel in your Apple Account settings, the same as any App Store subscription: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, FlirtGym, Cancel. Cancelling at least 24 hours before renewal stops the next charge. FlirtGym cannot cancel it for you, because Apple handles the billing.

How does FlirtGym's price compare to other AI dating apps?

It sits in the middle. RIZZ COACH starts around $3.99 per week, which is more per year than FlirtGym's annual plan. Several competitors run $20 to $30 per month. FlirtGym's annual plan at $59.99 is toward the cheaper end for the category; its monthly plan at $14.99 is about average.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Settings, Delete Account. This removes your account and signs you out of Apple and RevenueCat. It is a real deletion, not a deactivation.
