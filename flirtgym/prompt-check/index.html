The test this applies

Read your answer back and ask what would someone message me about this. If the answer is nothing, the prompt is decoration and it has wasted one of your three slots.

"Travel, food and good vibes" gives nobody anything. "I will defend gas station coffee in front of a jury" gives everybody something. The second is not cleverer. It is just answerable.

Questions

Does this work for Bumble and Tinder too?

Yes. The test is not platform specific. Any profile field that someone is meant to reply to either gives them something to say or it does not.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. It is JavaScript running on your device. Nothing is transmitted, logged or stored.

Why does it sometimes call a good answer decoration?

Because it reads text, not intent. An answer that is meaningful to you but names nothing specific and invites no reply will score as decoration, which is roughly how a stranger reads it too. If you disagree with the call, the useful question is what someone could actually send back.
