Foldbook Support

Frequently asked questions

Does Foldbook need an account?
No. There's no sign-in, no account — open the app and start reading the sample library, or import your own file.
How do I add my own book?
Tap import in your library and choose a file using the system file picker. It's added to your library on your device.
Is a file I import sent anywhere?
No. Foldbook has no account and no server — an imported file stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
What are the sample books in the library?
Short original stories written for the app, bundled in so there's something to read immediately. They aren't real published books.
Will my reading progress be saved?
Your theme and font size preferences are saved on your device. If your page position in a book doesn't hold between sessions, tell us which book and device — that's useful for us to know.
What file types can I import?
Whatever your device's file picker can select. If a specific file won't open, tell us its type and we'll check it.
Is there anything to buy?
No. Foldbook is free, with every feature unlocked — no subscription, no in-app purchase, no account.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and iOS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot. That usually points us straight at it.

Price

Foldbook is free, with no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no account. There’s nothing to buy, cancel, or refund.
