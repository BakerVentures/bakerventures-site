Small tools that do one thing

Updated September 6, 2026 · published by Baker Ventures LLC · sources cited inline

Baker Ventures LLC builds small tools that do one thing. Six products, six categories, and a website for each one publishing that category's real answers free, with sources attached and no signup.

Two of the six are actually available. shipcheck ships as a Claude Code plugin; FlirtGym has been on the App Store since 12 August 2026. The other four are in development, and every page about them says so.

The products

shipcheck — shippingCatch the App Store or Play rejection before you submit. Scans a React Native or Expo project inside Claude Code, cites the exact clause, names the file. Nothing uploaded anywhere. FlirtGym — live since 12 Aug 2026Practise the conversation before it is real. Screenshot a message and get three replies with the reason each one works. Formerly RizzMaxx. MileTruth — in developmentThe gig mileage tracker that does not miss trips and tells you what the driving actually paid after the car took its cut. Rigsheet — in developmentJob management for one-van detailing and cleaning businesses. Flat price, no per-seat fee, vehicle-size and home-size pricing built in. HormoneLog — in developmentAn HRT regimen and a symptom history in a form a clinician can actually read. Nearvo — in developmentA daily connection habit for couples. One subscription unlocks both partners.

Free tools, no signup

Every one of these works whether or not the app it belongs to ever ships.

  • The 2026 two-rate mileage calculator — 2026 has two IRS business mileage rates split at June 30. Most calculators still use one.
  • Apple's required-reason API reference — all five categories and all sixteen approved reason codes, transcribed from Apple's rendered documentation because that page returns an empty shell to a plain fetch.
  • The job pricing floor calculator — your costs and your real sellable hours produce the number below which a job loses you money.
  • Free conversation question sets — bids for connection, after a fight, moving in together, money.
  • What menopause symptom scales are — and what a score can and cannot tell you.

Open data

Apple privacy-manifest SDK → npm map — all 86 SDKs on Apple's third-party requirements list mapped to the React Native and Expo packages that pull each one in. 50 have an RN route; 32 of those arrive transitively, which is where ITMS-91061 rejections come from. CC BY 4.0, corrections welcome.

How this is written

Questions and answers

What is Baker Ventures?

Baker Ventures LLC is an independent one-person software studio building small, single-purpose iOS and developer tools. Its products are MileTruth, Rigsheet, HormoneLog, Nearvo, FlirtGym and shipcheck.

What apps does Baker Ventures make?

shipcheck, a Claude Code plugin checking React Native and Expo apps for App Store and Play rejection risk, and FlirtGym, an iOS dating conversation practice app formerly called RizzMaxx. MileTruth, Rigsheet, HormoneLog and Nearvo are in development.

Are Baker Ventures apps available now?

Two of six. shipcheck ships and FlirtGym has been on the App Store since 12 August 2026. The other four are in development and not released, and every page about them says so.

Where can I find Baker Ventures' free tools?

Each product has its own site with free tools and answers, no signup - a two-rate 2026 mileage calculator at miletruth, a job pricing calculator at rigsheet, the Apple required-reason API reference at shipcheck, and free question sets at nearvo.
