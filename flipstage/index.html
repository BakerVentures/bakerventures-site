Your notes on your half. The slide on theirs.

FlipStage splits one iPhone into two panes, so you can present across a table without a projector, a second screen or a laptop. You read your notes and watch the clock. They just see the slide.

FlipStage · an app by Baker Ventures LLC · free, no account

What it does

Two panes, one phone.Present mode divides the screen in two. The audience pane shows the current slide and a position badge. Your pane shows your speaker notes, the prev and next controls and the slide count. The panes stack in portrait and sit side by side in landscape.
Your slides are your photos.Import from your photo library with the standard iOS picker and each photo becomes a slide. Pick several at once.
Notes written where you will read them.Type talking points onto any slide before you start, and they appear on your half while you present.
Reorder before you walk in.Move a slide up or down the running order, or delete it.
A clock you can actually see.The elapsed timer starts with the presentation and pauses on a tap. Set a target of 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes and the time turns red once you pass it.

How it works

  1. Import your slides as photos from your library.
  2. Add notes and set the order.
  3. Hit Present and put the phone down between you.
Two things to know before you download. FlipStage keeps a deck for the current session only: nothing is saved, so when you close the app the deck is gone and you re-import next time. Your photos themselves are never moved or altered. And it imports photos, not documents, so a PDF or a Keynote file has to be exported as images first. Both are deliberate, and both are the first things we would change.

Privacy, in one line

FlipStage has no account, no server and no analytics, and the app contains no networking code, so there is nothing for your slides or notes to be sent over. Its only permission request is access to your photo library, and that is what the import button uses. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

What does Present actually do?
It divides the one screen into two panes: the slide and a slide counter on the audience pane, and your speaker notes, the prev and next controls and a timer on your pane. The panes stack in portrait and sit side by side in landscape.
Can I import a PDF, Keynote or PowerPoint file?
No. FlipStage imports photos from your library. Export your slides as images first, then import those.
Will my deck still be there next time I open the app?
No. FlipStage holds a deck for the current session only and nothing is written to storage. Re-import your slides the next time you present. Your original photos are untouched in your library either way.
Is anything I import sent anywhere?
No. FlipStage has no account and no server, and the app contains no networking code at all. Your photos and notes stay on the device.
Does it need an account?
No. There is no sign-in and no setup beyond importing your first slide.
Is there anything to buy?
No. FlipStage is free, with every feature included. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no account, so there is nothing to cancel or refund.
