Privacy policy

Hatch Window is a fly-fishing conditions and catch-log app published by Baker Ventures LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Last updated 14 October 2026

There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDK of any kind. We receive nothing from this app, and there is no server of ours for it to send anything to.

Two requests do leave your phone, and they are named in full in section 3. Your catch log is in your own device backup, on purpose, and that is section 2.

Section 1What we collect, how, and what it is used for

This app does not collect or transmit personal data to us. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDK of any kind. We receive nothing from this app, and there is no server of ours for it to send anything to.

Section 2Where your data lives

Everything you add (custom spots, catch and outing log entries, app settings) is stored in a local database on your device, is used solely to show it back to you inside the app, and is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app clears that database from the device, and there is no account or server copy of ours to also remove — but see the panel below, because your own backup is a separate copy that deleting the app does not touch.

The one exception is described in full in Section 3: to show current conditions, the app sends a river site number or a spot’s coordinates — including the coordinates of a spot you added yourself — to two public data services. Your notes, catches and settings are never part of those requests.

Your catch log is in your own device backup

Two different things are worth stating separately, because one blanket sentence about your data would get one of them wrong.

What the app sends: nothing to us, ever, and nothing but a river site number or a pair of coordinates to the two public services named in Section 3. Your spots’ names, your catches, your notes and your settings are never sent anywhere by this app.

What your device backup holds: all of it. iOS backs up an app’s local files to iCloud by default, and Hatch Window’s database goes into the backup whole — the app does nothing to hold any of it back. If iCloud Backup is on, your custom spots — including their coordinates — your catch and outing log, and your settings are in your iCloud backup. Measured on a running build rather than assumed: the directory holding the database was checked, and nothing marks it to be held back.

That backup is in your Apple account, not ours: it is encrypted, we have no access to it, and we cannot read it. We still hold no copy of your data. We left the data in the backup deliberately, because a season’s worth of spots and outings is exactly the thing a lost phone should not take with it. If you would rather it were not there, you can turn Hatch Window off under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups.

Section 3Network requests this app makes

To show current conditions, the app requests public, non-personal data directly from two free government/public services over HTTPS:

  • U.S. Geological Survey (usgs.gov) — streamflow and gauge height for a river site number, from the USGS public water data service. USGS is retiring its older water-services host and moving to a replacement, so which USGS endpoint this app calls depends on the app version you are running — but the recipient, the request, and the single river site number sent are identical either way.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — current weather for a latitude/longitude.

These requests send only the river site number or coordinates needed to answer the request — never your name, device identifier, location history, or any other personal information. We don’t control what each service’s own infrastructure logs (for example, your IP address, as with any web request); neither service receives anything from us to associate that with an identity.

Section 4Who we share your data with

No one. The app does not track you and does not share data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose, because it does not collect any data to share. The two public services named in Section 3 receive only a river site number or a pair of coordinates — no personal information of any kind — so there is no user data held by a third party whose protection we would need to confirm. If a future version ever sends personal data to a provider, this policy will name that provider and state that it is contractually bound to protect your data to at least the standard set out here, and will be updated before that version ships.

Section 5Data retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

  • Retention. Your spots, log entries, and settings remain on your device until you delete them, and in your own device backup for as long as you keep that backup. Baker Ventures LLC holds no copy and has no retention period of its own.
  • Deletion. Delete individual entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device. There is no account to close and no company-held record to request the deletion of. A backup taken earlier can still hold the data until you delete that backup too.
  • Withdrawing consent. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no ongoing processing by us to withdraw consent for.

Section 6Children

Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

Section 7Changes to this policy

If what this app collects or contacts ever changes, this page will be updated first, in the same app update that makes the change.
