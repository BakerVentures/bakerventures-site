Frequently asked questions

Does FlipStage need an account?
No. There's no sign-in, no account, and no setup beyond importing your first slide.
How do I import slides?
Tap import and choose photos from your library using the system picker — each photo becomes a slide you can reorder and attach notes to.
Will my deck still be there next time I open the app?
No — FlipStage keeps your deck for your current session only. Re-import your slides the next time you present. Your original photos are untouched in your library either way.
Is anything I import sent anywhere?
No. FlipStage has no account and no server — your photos and notes stay on your device the whole time. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
What does 'Present' actually do?
It splits your screen into two panes on the same device: your notes and controls on your side, the slide upright for whoever is sitting across from you.
Can I import PDFs or slide decks directly?
Not yet — FlipStage currently imports photos. Export your deck's slides as images first, then import them.
Is there anything to buy?
No. FlipStage is free, with every feature unlocked — no subscription, no in-app purchase, no account.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and iOS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot. That usually points us straight at it.

Price

FlipStage is free, with no in-app purchase, no subscription, and no account. There’s nothing to buy, cancel, or refund.
