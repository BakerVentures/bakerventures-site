The FlirtGym free trial, where offered

How a trial works on the App Store, who decides if you get one, and how to cancel.

Last updated 2026-10-13

FlirtGym Pro offers a free trial where Apple shows one to you. Eligibility is decided per user by the App Store, so a trial appears on the subscription screen only if you qualify. There is no free tier in the app. You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Get FlirtGym

Free to download. FlirtGym Pro is $4.99/wk, $9.99/mo or $39.99/yr. Any trial and the price that follows it are shown on Apple's purchase screen before you confirm. Cancel in Apple Account settings.

What a trial includes

If you are offered a trial, it is Pro, not a reduced version of it.

  • Unlimited practice conversations with AI characters, across texting, dating apps and in-person scenarios
  • Every message you write scored, with the specific reason it landed where it did
  • The in-session Coach, which gives you a hint or the exact line, privately
  • Rate My Profile and Rate My Look
  • Every scenario, including cold approach
  • The full lesson and challenge track

Who is eligible

Apple decides. Introductory offers are limited by Apple's own rules, including one per person per subscription group, so someone who has used one before may be shown the plain price. If the subscription screen shows no trial, none applies to you. FlirtGym does not advertise an offer the store will not honour.

How to cancel

Apple handles the billing, so this happens in iOS Settings and not in the app. It takes under a minute, and you can do it at any time.

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Tap FlirtGym
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

If you are in a trial, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid the first charge. You keep access until the period you have already started finishes. If something goes wrong, support is one inbox and a person reads it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does FlirtGym have a free trial?

Where offered. FlirtGym is a subscription app, and there is no free tier. Whether a free trial is available is decided per user by Apple: the App Store only shows a trial on the subscription screen if you qualify for it. If you do not see a trial there, none is available to you and the price shown is what you pay.

Who is eligible for a trial?

Apple decides. Introductory offers on the App Store are limited by Apple's own eligibility rules, for example one per person per subscription group, so someone who has used one before may be shown the plain price. FlirtGym cannot change that, and the app does not advertise an offer the store will not honour.

How do I cancel before I am charged?

On your iPhone: Settings, tap your name at the top, Subscriptions, FlirtGym, Cancel Subscription. If a trial is running, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can cancel at any time. FlirtGym cannot cancel it for you, because Apple owns the billing relationship.

What happens when a trial ends?

If you started a subscription with a trial and did not cancel, it converts to the paid plan you chose, charged to your Apple Account. The price and billing period are shown on Apple's purchase screen before you confirm.

Can I try FlirtGym without subscribing?

The practice conversations, message scoring and coaching are all part of Pro. The browser tools on this site are free, with no account and no payment method, and they let you test the scoring without installing anything.
