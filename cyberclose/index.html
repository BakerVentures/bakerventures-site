Be ready before you fill out the cyber-insurance application.

cyberclose walks a small business through the security controls insurers actually ask about — so you know where you stand and what to fix before you apply or renew.

cyberclose · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

The questions insurers really ask.Work through a readiness assessment built around the controls that show up on cyber-insurance applications — MFA, backups, access, patching, and more.
See your gaps, ranked.Get a clear readiness picture that highlights the missing controls most likely to matter, so you fix the important ones first.
Plain-English fix guidance.Each gap comes with a straightforward explanation of what the control is and practical steps to close it — no security degree required.
A report you can act on.Export your readiness summary as a PDF to guide your team, your IT provider, or your conversation with a broker.
Re-check before renewal.Run the assessment again before each renewal to confirm you've held or improved your posture.

How it works

  1. Answer the readiness questions about your current controls.
  2. Review your gaps, ranked by what matters most.
  3. Export the report and work the fixes before you apply or renew.

Questions

Does cyberclose get me approved or insured?
No. It helps you assess and improve readiness; underwriting and coverage decisions are made by insurers. It does not sell, bind, or guarantee any policy.
Is this a security audit or a guarantee I'm secure?
No. It is a self-assessment against commonly requested controls — a strong starting point, not a penetration test or a guarantee against a breach.
Is this insurance or legal advice?
No. cyberclose is an educational readiness tool. For coverage decisions talk to a licensed broker; for legal questions, an attorney.
Who is it for?
Small and mid-sized businesses without a dedicated security team who want to understand what insurers expect.
Does it connect to my systems?
No. Your answers are self-reported; the app does not scan or access your network.
Is my business data private?
Your assessment data is stored for your use and is not sold. See the in-app privacy policy.
Please note. cyberclose is an educational readiness tool. It is not insurance, legal, or security-audit advice. It scores your readiness from your own self-reported answers against published frameworks, which makes it a structured estimate of where you stand — not a security audit, not a penetration test, and not a guarantee against a breach. It does not sell, bind, or guarantee any policy, and a high readiness score does not mean an insurer will approve you or lower your premium: underwriting is the insurer's decision, made on their own application, which is what governs your coverage. Confirm anything you rely on with your IT or security lead, and talk to a licensed broker about coverage and an attorney about legal questions.
