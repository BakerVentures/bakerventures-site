Small tools that do one thing

Updated October 14, 2026 · published by Baker Ventures LLC · every app status read from App Store Connect the same day

Baker Ventures LLC builds small tools that do one thing. One person, nineteen products, and a website for each one publishing that category’s real answers free, with sources attached and no signup.

Six of them you can install today. shipcheck ships as a Claude Code plugin; FlirtGym, FlipStage, Hatch Window, MoveWitness and Vocabdrift are on the App Store. The other thirteen are not on sale yet, and no date for them appears anywhere on this site, because Apple sets release dates and does not tell us in advance. The apps page carries the status of every one.

Free tools, no signup
