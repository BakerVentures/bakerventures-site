Please note. lienclock is an organizing, calculating, and reminder tool — not a law firm — and it does not provide legal advice. It reports what the statute says; it does not tell you what to do and it does not file anything on your behalf. Lien law is complex and state-specific, and statutory periods have exceptions for holidays, weekends, and disputed first-work dates. Treat every date the app shows as a working estimate, and confirm anything you rely on with a construction attorney in your state before you act on it.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the deadlines come from?
Each is computed from your state's current mechanics-lien statute. Every deadline card shows the section number and quoted text, with a link to read it yourself.
Is lienclock legal advice?
No. It's a calculator and reference tool. It reports what the statute says; it doesn't tell you what to do or file anything on your behalf. Confirm anything critical with an attorney.
Why does a deadline say “missing information”?
That deadline depends on a date you haven't entered yet. Rather than show a number that could be wrong, lienclock tells you what it needs.
Which states are covered?
All 50 states plus the District of Columbia, each modeled from that state's own lien statute with the citation it's based on.
Do you keep my job data?
No account, no server. Every job stays on your device. If you delete the app, the data goes with it.
Can I get reminders?
Yes — Pro schedules local reminders ahead of each deadline. They run on your device and need notification permission. If one didn't fire, check that Settings › Notifications › lienclock is allowed and that the job has a start/anchor date entered — the app can't count from a date it doesn't have.
What does Pro unlock?
Reminders, statutory-form document generation, calendar export, and unlimited jobs. Viewing computed deadlines is free.
The generated document has blanks — is that a bug?
No. Anything the statute doesn't let us fill from your entered dates is left as a clear [FILL IN] blank, on purpose. We never guess a legal value.
A deadline looks wrong for my situation.
Statutes have exceptions lienclock may not model for an edge case, and real deadlines can turn on details the app doesn't know — your contract type, notices sent, project and role. Read the cited section, confirm with a construction attorney in your state, and if it still looks off, email us the state, role, and dates and we'll re-check our rule against the statute.
How do I restore my purchase?
Your unlock is tied to the store account you bought with. Open lienclock › Settings or the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you used originally. That should bring it back — if not, send us a screenshot of what you see and we'll dig in.
How do I cancel, or ask a billing question?
Billing runs through the store, not us, so we can't cancel or change a charge on our end. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › lienclock. Android: Play Store › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the period ends.
How do I get a refund?
We don't process the payment — Apple handles App Store refunds — so the quickest route is reportaproblem.apple.com (Android: play.google.com/store/account): pick the purchase and choose “Request a refund.” If the store gives you any trouble, tell us and we'll help where we can.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and OS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot with anything sensitive blurred. That usually points us straight at it.
