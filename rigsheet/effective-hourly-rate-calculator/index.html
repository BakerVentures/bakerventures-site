Why on-site hours lie

Booking Koala makes the same point: divide a flat package price by hours and the inconsistencies between packages show up fast. The honest denominator is the whole block of your day the job used, which is on-site time plus the drive plus the work around it. Read their breakdown for the flat-fee example.

How it is calculated

  • Costs: materials, miles times cost per mile, and the card fee (percent of the price plus the fixed amount).
  • Take-home is price plus tip minus those costs. Divided by on-site plus drive plus prep hours, that is the all-in hourly.
  • Required price solves for your target: (target hourly x total hours + materials + mileage - tip + fixed fee) / (1 - fee%).
  • Overhead such as insurance and software is not in here. For a price built from overhead and margin use the job price calculator.
Take-home here is before income tax and self-employment tax. This page does arithmetic; it does not tell you what to charge or what you owe.
