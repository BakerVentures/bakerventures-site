What Vocabdrift is — learning, not expertise

Vocabdrift is an English-vocabulary learning app. It rotates a word, its part of speech and a short definition on your Lock Screen, optionally sends you the same word as a local notification, and quizzes you on the vocabulary with a multiple-choice quiz. That is the entire product.

It is a general-education tool for your own learning, and nothing more. As a term of this agreement:

  • It is not an authority, and definitions are not exhaustive. Each entry shows one short sense of a word, chosen to be learnable on a Lock Screen. Words have senses, registers, connotations and usage constraints that a one-line definition cannot carry. Don't rely on Vocabdrift for anything that turns on a word's precise meaning — translation, drafting, an exam answer, or an argument about what a word means.

  • No guarantee the words appear. The Lock Screen widget and local notifications are scheduled through the operating system, which decides when — and whether — to show them. iOS grants a widget only a limited number of refreshes a day and holds a limited number of pending notifications, and adding the widget to your Lock Screen is a step only you can do. A word not appearing is usually the OS, not a defect we can fix.
