Reconcile your trust account in minutes, not evenings.

iolta walks a solo or small-firm attorney through a clean three-way reconciliation — bank, book, and client ledgers agreeing to the penny — every month.

iolta · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

True three-way match.iolta reconciles your bank statement, your trust ledger, and the sum of every client's individual ledger — and flags the moment the three don't agree.
Per-client ledgers that never go negative.Track each client's balance so you can see at a glance that no client's funds were ever overdrawn — the error bar exams warn about.
Find the difference fast.When the three don't tie out, iolta highlights outstanding items and uncleared transactions so you're hunting the gap, not re-adding columns.
A record you can hand to an auditor.Produce a dated three-way reconciliation report as a PDF, month after month, so your file is ready if the bar ever asks.
Built for the small firm.No enterprise onboarding — enter your accounts, import or type transactions, and reconcile the same way each month.

How it works

  1. Set up your trust account and client ledgers — one ledger per client, and per matter if you choose.
  2. Enter or import the month's transactions and mark what's cleared the bank.
  3. Reconcile — iolta ties the three balances together and produces a report, or shows you exactly where they differ.

Questions

Does iolta connect to my bank automatically?
The app is built around entering or importing your statement data; see the app for current import options. It does not move money.
Does this make me compliant with my state bar?
No. iolta helps you perform and document a three-way reconciliation, but trust-accounting rules vary by state and compliance is your professional responsibility. It is not legal or accounting advice.
Can it handle multiple client matters?
Yes. You keep a separate ledger per client, and per matter if you choose, which roll up into the trust total.
What if my reconciliation doesn't balance?
iolta shows the outstanding and uncleared items driving the difference so you can find and correct the entry.
Is my client financial data secure?
Your data is stored for your use in the app and is not sold. Review the in-app privacy policy and your duty of confidentiality before entering client details.
Do I need special accounting knowledge?
You should understand basic trust-accounting duties. iolta structures the reconciliation but does not replace your training or your accountant.
Please note. iolta is a calculator and record-keeping tool. It is not legal or accounting advice, and it never states that your account is compliant with any jurisdiction's rule — it reports whether your three balances tie out, and if not, where they don't. Trust-accounting rules vary by state and compliance remains your professional responsibility; your state bar's rules are the authority, and a mismatch you can't explain is worth reviewing with your bar's resources or an accountant. iolta does not move, hold, or transfer money. Review your own duty of confidentiality before entering client details.
