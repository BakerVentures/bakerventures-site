172 deadlines across 51 jurisdictions (50 states and DC), each with the statute it was read from. Pick a state below, or use the calculator.

Information, not legal advice. This app reports what the statute says and cites the section it read. It is not legal advice, it does not file anything for you, and it does not tell you what to do.
Verify with the statute or an attorney. Statutes change, and courts reinterpret them. Every deadline in this app comes from a statute read on the date shown next to its citation - that snapshot can go out of date. Missing a mechanics lien deadline can extinguish your claim permanently, so confirm any deadline you are relying on with an attorney licensed in that state before you act on it.

About the NoticeGuard app

NoticeGuard is an app that turns a job's dates into a countdown for each statutory step, shows the statute text each date was read from, exports a calendar file (.ics) and sends reminders 14, 7 and 1 day before each deadline. It tracks and reminds; it does not file anything for you.

A free tool from Baker Ventures LLC. Information, not legal advice. The pages are generated from the rule data inside the NoticeGuard app (source commit 22f9300), so the figures here are the figures the app uses.
