Why the first photo carries almost everything

The decision is made in about a second, on one image, with no other information. Everything else in your profile only gets looked at by people the first photo already interested.

That makes it the highest-leverage thing you own and the most common place profiles fail. A group photo, sunglasses, a distant shot or bad lighting each costs you people who would otherwise have swiped.

Questions

Do I upload anything?

No. You describe the lineup with a few dropdowns. There is no upload, no image processing and nothing transmitted, which also means this cannot judge lighting or composition.

Why only four to six photos?

Because the weakest photo drags the average down harder than the best one lifts it. Padding a set out with photos you are unsure about costs more than the extra slot gains.

Does the order really matter that much?

The first one does, disproportionately. After that, what matters more is variety across the set than the exact sequence.
