Never miss a mechanics lien deadline again.

NoticeGuard turns your job's key dates into a running countdown — preliminary notice, notice of intent, and lien filing — so you keep your right to get paid.

NoticeGuard · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

Every deadline, counting down.Enter a job's first-furnishing or contract date and NoticeGuard shows the days remaining to each statutory step, sorted by what's due next.
State-aware windows.Pick the state the job is in and the app applies that state's notice and filing periods, so a job in one state doesn't inherit another's clock.
Reminders before it's too late.Get on-device alerts ahead of each deadline — early enough to send a notice or file, not the morning it expires.
One place for every active job.Track all your open jobs at once with a dashboard that surfaces the nearest deadline across your whole book of work.
Your record, exportable.Keep dates, parties, and amounts per job and export a clean summary as a PDF for your files or your attorney.

How it works

  1. Add the job — property, parties, contract or first-work date, and state.
  2. See the clock — NoticeGuard lays out each deadline with days remaining.
  3. Act on the reminder — get alerted before each window closes and mark each step done as you send or file.

Questions

Does NoticeGuard file the lien for me?
No. It tracks and reminds you of deadlines; you or your attorney prepare and file the actual documents with the county or court.
Is this legal advice?
No. NoticeGuard is an organizing and reminder tool, not a law firm, and does not provide legal advice. Confirm any deadline with a construction attorney before relying on it.
Which states are supported?
The app applies notice and filing periods for the states listed in the app; other states can be tracked with custom manual dates.
What if my deadline calculation is wrong?
Statutory periods have exceptions for holidays, weekends, and disputed first-work dates. Treat dates as a working estimate and verify critical deadlines independently.
Does my job data leave my phone?
Job records are stored for your use in the app and are not sold. See the in-app privacy policy for specifics.
Do I need a subscription?
NoticeGuard offers a subscription for tracking multiple active jobs, with pricing shown in-app.
Please note. NoticeGuard is an organizing, calculating, and reminder tool — not a law firm — and it does not provide legal advice. It reports what the statute says; it does not tell you what to do and it does not file anything on your behalf. Lien law is complex and state-specific, and statutory periods have exceptions for holidays, weekends, and disputed first-work dates. Treat every date the app shows as a working estimate, and confirm anything you rely on with a construction attorney in your state before you act on it.
