1. Not legal or accounting advice

The app tracks and reconciles. It does not advise. Specifically:

  • It is not legal advice, and using it creates no attorney-client or any other professional relationship between you and Baker Ventures LLC.
  • It is not accounting advice, not bookkeeping services, and not an audit. No accountant reviews your entries.

The app deliberately does not hard-code a reconciliation mandate or frequency for any jurisdiction: the one jurisdiction rule entry it ships (Utah) quotes the rule verbatim precisely because that rule does not itself state a three-way reconciliation requirement — see docs/CITATION-VERIFICATION.md. Three-way reconciliation is standard trust-accounting discipline; the app does not claim it is your jurisdiction's stated requirement.

2. Accuracy is yours

The app computes from exactly what you enter. It cannot see your bank, does not connect to any financial institution, and cannot verify a statement balance, a date, or an amount. A reconciliation that ties out proves the numbers you entered are internally consistent — nothing more. Always check the app's figures against your actual bank statement and your own records before relying on them, and keep your authoritative trust records independently of this app (section 6).

The app refuses to record a disbursement, fee, or transfer that would take an individual client ledger negative, because one client's funds must never cover another's. That check is a safeguard on data entry, not a guarantee that your trust account is in order.

3. No account; your data stays on your device

The app requires no account, no sign-in, and no personal details, and it makes no network requests. Everything you enter — trust accounts, client ledgers, transactions, statement figures, saved reconciliations — is stored only on your own device. See docs/privacy-policy.md for the full detail.

Use the app on a device you control and secure, and be deliberate about where an exported report goes — it can contain client names, matters, and amounts.

5. Exports
