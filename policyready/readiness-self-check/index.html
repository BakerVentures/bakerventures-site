Cybersecurity readiness self-check

Answer for each control: Yes, Partial or No. The score and the ranked gap list update as you go.

A readiness self-check, not an audit. This is a structured self-report against published guidance. It is not a security audit, a penetration test, a certification or a compliance determination, and nothing here promises a particular insurance outcome. Underwriting is each insurer's decision, made on that insurer's own application. For coverage questions talk to a licensed broker; for security work, to your IT or security lead.

How the score works, as the app computes it: each control has a weight of 1, 2 or 3. Yes earns the full weight, Partial earns half, No and unanswered earn nothing. The score is the weighted total as a percent, rounded. Gaps are listed heaviest first. It runs in your browser, stores nothing and sends nothing. The result is a self-reported estimate of where you stand; it says nothing about whether any insurer will offer coverage or at what terms.
