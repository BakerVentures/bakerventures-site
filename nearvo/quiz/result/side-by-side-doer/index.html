A connection style

The Side-by-Side Doer

You feel closest when you are doing something together.

For you, closeness shows up in action: cooking together, tackling a project, a walk, or a partner who quietly takes something off your plate. Being useful to each other, or simply busy side by side, feels like love.

Worth knowing: help is not the only way your partner may be trying to show care. Look for what sits behind the gesture.

Three things to try this week, together

  1. Choose one small project this week, like a recipe, a shelf or a playlist, and do it together from start to finish.
  2. Take over one of your partner’s least favorite chores this week without being asked.
  3. Plan one outing and split the planning: one picks where, the other picks what to bring.

A question to ask your partner tonight

What is one thing I could take off your plate this week that would make a real difference?
