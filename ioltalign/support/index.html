Frequently asked questions

What is three-way reconciliation?
Comparing three totals that should all match: your adjusted bank balance, your book/register balance, and the sum of every client's ledger. IOLTAlign shows all three and names any discrepancy.
Is this legal or accounting advice?
No. It's a calculator and record-keeping tool. It never states your account is compliant with any jurisdiction's rule — it reports whether your numbers tie, and if not, where they don't. Your state bar's trust rules are the authority.
Does IOLTAlign move or hold money?
No. It records transactions you enter. It never touches an actual bank account or transfers funds.
How does it handle outstanding checks and deposits in transit?
You enter your bank statement's ending balance; IOLTAlign adjusts for outstanding items to produce the adjusted bank balance it compares against your books.
Why was my disbursement refused?
It would have driven that client's ledger below zero — one client's funds covering another's. That's blocked at entry by design.
Where is my client data stored?
On your device only. No account, no cloud. Your client names, ledgers, and balances never leave your phone and we have no way to see them. Deleting the app deletes the data — export first if you need a record.
Can I track more than one trust account?
Yes, with Pro. Each account keeps its own client ledgers and reconciliation history.
How often should I reconcile?
Most bar rules require monthly. Pro can remind you; the app doesn't decide your obligation — check your jurisdiction's rule.
Can I import transactions from my bank?
Not right now — IOLTAlign is a manual, on-device tool, so you enter transactions yourself. That's part of how your data stays entirely on your phone. Import is on the list to weigh; for now, entering as you go tends to catch discrepancies earlier anyway.
What's in the exported report?
The period, all three balances, the discrepancy breakdown, and per-client balances — formatted to share or file.
I found a discrepancy I can't explain.
IOLTAlign shows where the numbers diverge, not why. Review the transactions in that client's ledger against your bank statement; the cause is usually a missing or mis-tagged entry. If you think the app's math itself is off, send us a small example of the numbers and we'll check it.
How do I restore my purchase?
Your unlock is tied to the store account you bought with. Open IOLTAlign › Settings or the paywall and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID or Google account you used originally. That should bring it back — if not, send us a screenshot of what you see and we'll dig in.
How do I cancel, or ask a billing question?
Billing runs through the store, not us, so we can't cancel or change a charge on our end. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › IOLTAlign. Android: Play Store › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the period ends.
How do I get a refund?
We don't process the payment — Apple handles App Store refunds — so the quickest route is reportaproblem.apple.com (Android: play.google.com/store/account): pick the purchase and choose “Request a refund.” If the store gives you any trouble, tell us and we'll help where we can.
Something's broken. How do I report it?
Email us with: (1) your device and OS version, (2) what you were doing, (3) what you expected versus what happened, and (4) a screenshot with anything sensitive blurred. That usually points us straight at it.
