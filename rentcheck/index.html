Document the place before you lose your deposit over it.

MoveWitness turns a move-in or move-out walkthrough into timestamped, room-by-room photo evidence and a single PDF you can send if the deposit is ever disputed.

MoveWitness · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

Room-by-room, nothing missed.A guided checklist walks you through each room so you photograph the spots deposits actually get withheld over — walls, floors, appliances, fixtures.
Timestamped, dated proof.Every photo is captured with its date so there's a clear record of the unit's condition on the day you moved in or out.
Move-in vs. move-out, side by side.Do a matching walkthrough at both ends of the lease and compare the same rooms to show what did — and didn't — change.
One PDF you can actually send.Export the whole walkthrough as a clean, organized PDF report to email your landlord, property manager, or small-claims file.
Add your own notes.Attach a short note to any photo — “scuff was here at move-in” — so the evidence explains itself later.

How it works

  1. Create a walkthrough for the unit and pick move-in or move-out.
  2. Photograph each room following the checklist and add notes where it matters.
  3. Export the PDF and keep it — or send it the moment a deposit question comes up.

Questions

Will this guarantee I get my deposit back?
No. MoveWitness helps you create clear, dated evidence; whether a deposit is returned depends on your lease, your landlord, and your local law. It does not guarantee an outcome.
Is this legal advice?
No. It is a documentation tool, not a lawyer. For a contested deposit or a court filing, consult a tenant-rights resource or attorney in your area.
Are the photos time-stamped in a way a court accepts?
The report records the capture date for each photo. Evidentiary weight is up to the decision-maker; MoveWitness organizes your record but can't speak to how any specific court treats it.
Do my photos upload to a server?
No. The walkthrough lives on your device for your use; the PDF is one you generate and share yourself. See the privacy policy.
Can a landlord use it too?
Yes — the same walkthrough and report work for landlords documenting a unit's condition at turnover.
What if I forgot to document at move-in?
You can still do a move-out walkthrough; it's simply stronger when you have both ends to compare.
Please note. MoveWitness is a documentation tool, not a law firm, and nothing on this page or in the app is legal advice. Landlord-tenant and deposit rules vary a lot by state and city. For a contested deposit or a court filing, consult a qualified attorney or your local housing authority. The app's job is to make sure you have a clear, dated record to bring them.
