How to ask a girl out over text

Specific, timed, and phrased as a statement. Vagueness asks her to do the planning.

Last updated 2026-08-20

Propose something specific, with a time, as a statement rather than a request for permission. "There's a place near you doing tacos on Thursday, come with me" is far easier to answer than "would you maybe want to hang out sometime?" Vagueness asks her to do the planning and gives her nothing to say yes to. Ask earlier than feels natural.

Why specific beats open-ended

An open invitation transfers all the work to her: what, when, where, and whether you actually meant it. A specific proposal requires one decision.

It also signals something. A concrete plan reads as someone who decided he wanted to see her. "We should hang out sometime" reads as someone testing whether it would be embarrassing to ask.

The shape that works

  • An activity. Specific enough to picture.
  • A time. A day, not "sometime". Two options at most — more becomes scheduling.
  • Statement framing. "Come with me" over "would you want to maybe".
  • Short and low stakes. A drink for an hour, not dinner and a show.

When to ask

Sooner than most people do. Usually within a few days of matching, once the conversation is going reasonably.

The instinct to keep texting until it feels certain is understandable and usually counterproductive. Certainty does not arrive over text, and long exchanges lose momentum — a conversation that never becomes a plan almost always just stops.

If she says no

Take it in one message and move on. Do not ask why, do not offer alternatives twice, do not go quiet in a way she is supposed to notice.

A clean no handled gracefully sometimes becomes a yes weeks later. A no handled badly never does, and the difference costs you nothing but the urge to say one more thing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ask a girl out over text?

Propose something specific with a time attached, and phrase it as a statement rather than a request for permission. 'There's a place near you doing tacos on Thursday, come with me' is easier to say yes to than 'would you maybe want to hang out sometime?'

Is it bad to ask a girl out over text?

No. It is now the normal way it happens, especially with someone you met on an app. The old advice about calling is from a period when texting was not the default channel for everything else in the relationship either.

When should you ask her out?

Earlier than most people do. Once the conversation is going reasonably well — usually within a few days of matching. Long text exchanges tend to lose momentum, and a conversation that never becomes a plan usually just ends.

What if she says no or does not answer?

Take it cleanly. One light acknowledgement and move on; do not ask why, do not negotiate, and do not follow up twice. Handling a no well occasionally turns into a yes later, and handling it badly never does.

What should the first date be?

Short, cheap, and easy to leave. A drink or coffee for an hour beats dinner, because neither of you is trapped if it is not working and it is easy to extend if it is.
