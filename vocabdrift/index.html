Build a bigger vocabulary a few minutes a day.

Vocabdrift uses short, spaced review sessions to teach new words and bring back the ones you're about to forget — so what you learn actually sticks.

Vocabdrift · an app by Baker Ventures LLC

What it does

Spaced repetition that sticks.Vocabdrift schedules each word to come back right before you'd forget it, so you keep more with less time.
Short sessions that fit your day.Learn in a few-minute session — on a break, in line, before bed — instead of a marathon you'll skip.
Words in real context.See each word used in a clear example, not just a definition, so you learn how to actually use it.
Track what's sticking.Watch your known-words grow and see which ones still need work, so progress is visible and motivating.
Learn at your level.Focus on the words worth your time, and mark the ones you already know so you're not drilling what you've got.

How it works

  1. Start a session and meet a few new words in context.
  2. Review with quick recall prompts as Vocabdrift schedules each word's next appearance.
  3. Come back daily — a few minutes keeps the streak and the words.

Questions

How is Vocabdrift different from flashcards?
It schedules reviews with spaced repetition and shows words in context, so you're reminded at the right time instead of flipping the same deck.
How much time does it take?
It's built for short daily sessions of a few minutes; consistency matters more than length.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Your words are stored on your device and rotate offline — there's no account and no server call to see them.
Is it for a specific test or language level?
You can pick from packs like SAT, GRE, TOEFL, and everyday vocabulary; Pro unlocks all of them.
Does it track my data?
Your progress is stored on your device to schedule reviews, and is not sold. See the privacy policy.
Is there a free way to try it?
Vocabdrift lets you start learning and offers a subscription for full access; pricing is shown in-app.
Please note. Definitions come from a curated set, and mistakes do slip through. If a word or definition looks wrong, tell us the exact word and what looks off — we'll verify it and fix it in an update.
